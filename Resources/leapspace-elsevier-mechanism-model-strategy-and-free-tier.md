## Summary

LeapSpace is Elsevier's AI-assisted research workspace, launched in 2025 as a "research-grade" alternative to general AI chatbots [@unknown2025]. It is not the same product as the domain leapspace.ai, which currently hosts a waitlist for "Orian," an unrelated community-intelligence tool. The research platform lives at elsevier.com/products/leapspace.

The mechanism is retrieval-augmented generation over a curated scholarly corpus: 100+ million Scopus-indexed records plus 23+ million full-text articles from Elsevier and content partners (BMJ Group, IOP Publishing, NEJM Group, Sage Publishing, 1,000+ providers) [@unknown2026]. It layers agentic AI, reasoning engines, and generative AI on this corpus, producing Deep Research reports, compare tables, Writing Coach feedback, funding and expert discovery, and Trust Cards that display the exact passage supporting each claim. Retracted articles are excluded, ranking is publisher-neutral, and an independent AI Advisory Board oversees model evaluation [@unknown2026].

On model provenance, LeapSpace uses a multi-model approach: Elsevier selects among AI models per task rather than relying on one in-house foundation model [@unknown2025]. Users cannot bring their own model or API key; BYOK is not offered, and access is gated by subscription rather than credits.

The free tier is real: a €0 plan with no credit card required offers unlimited LeapSpace Search, unlimited Deep Research, Writing Coach, file upload, funding and expert discovery, Trust Cards, and compare tables [@unknown2026c]. Paid individual access costs about $32 per month, and institutions can license organization-wide access [@unknown2025a].

## Key Points

- Mechanism: RAG over curated peer-reviewed content (Scopus + licensed full text), with traceable citations and human oversight.
- Model: multi-model selection per task; no proprietary foundation model advertised.
- BYOK: not supported; no user-supplied API keys.
- Free tier: €0 plan with unlimited search and Deep Research; paid individual plan ~$32/month; institutional licensing available.
- Caveat: leapspace.ai (Orian) is a different, unrelated product.
