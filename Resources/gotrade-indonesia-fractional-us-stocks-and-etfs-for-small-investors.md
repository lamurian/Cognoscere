## Summary

GoTrade Indonesia is a mobile-first brokerage app that enables Indonesian investors to buy US stocks and ETFs with fractional shares starting from $1. It launched globally in 2021, and entered the Indonesian market legally in 2023 through a partnership with PT Valbury Asia Futures, a Bappebti-licensed futures broker (license 184/BAPPEBTI/SI/II/2003). GoTrade has over 600,000 Indonesian users and offers access to 600+ US stocks and 50+ US ETFs. It is rated 4.7/5 on both Google Play Store (8,000+ reviews) and Apple App Store (2,400+ reviews). For users investing IDR 800,000/month (~$45), GoTrade is the most cost-effective international option.

## ETF Availability

GoTrade offers 50+ US ETFs including popular options tracking the S&P 500 (SPY, VOO), Nasdaq (QQQ), and other major indices. ETFs can be purchased fractionally — investors can buy as little as $1 worth of any ETF. This makes it ideal for small monthly contributions. Users cannot buy Indonesian ETFs or stocks through GoTrade — it is purely for US markets.

## Fee Breakdown

**Trading fees:**
- Regular user: 0.3% per transaction (min $0.10)
- Prestige user: 0.2% per transaction (min $0.10)
- JFX fee: 0.05% (max $0.10 per transaction)
- PPN 11% on (trading fee + JFX fee)
- SEC fee: ~$0.0000206 per $1 (sell only, min $0.01)
- TAF (FINRA): ~$0.000195 per share (sell only, min $0.01)
- CAT (FINRA): ~$0.000003 per share (min $0.01)

**Real cost example** (regular user, buy $45 worth of VOO):
- Trading fee: max($45 × 0.3%, $0.10) = $0.10
- JFX fee: min($45 × 0.05%, $0.10) = $0.0225
- PPN 11%: ($0.10 + $0.0225) × 11% = $0.0135
- Total buy cost: ~$0.14 (0.3% of $45)

**Sell would add:** SEC $0.01 + TAF $0.01 + CAT $0.01 ≈ $0.03 extra

**Topup/deposit fees:**
- Bank transfer (Virtual Account): FREE
- E-wallet (GoPay, OVO, DANA, ShopeePay): Rp 5,750
- USD deposit (if you already have USD): FREE
- FX spread during live market (09:00-15:00 WIB): 0.25%-0.35%
- FX spread when market closed: bank exchange rate + spread

**Withdrawal fees:**
- Withdrawal fee: $2 per withdrawal
- FX spread on withdrawal: 0.25%-0.35% (live market) or bank rate + spread (closed)

**Other fees:**
- US dividend withholding tax: 15%

**Total estimated cost per $45 monthly buy: ~$0.14 (0.3%)**

**Total estimated cost per $45 monthly sell: ~$0.17 (0.38%) + $2 withdrawal fee**

So for a monthly $45 ETF purchase with quarterly withdrawal (e.g., after 3 months = $135):
- Buy fees: 3 × $0.14 = $0.42
- Sell fee: $135 × 0.3% = $0.41 (or min $0.10) = $0.41 + SEC/TAF/CAT ~$0.03 + JFX + PPN
- Withdrawal: $2
- Total round-trip for 3 months: ~$2.86 on $135 = 2.1%

For buy-and-hold (no sell, no withdrawal), monthly cost is just the buy fee: ~$0.14/$45 = 0.3% per month.

## Topup Methods

GoTrade supports:
- Bank transfer via Virtual Account — FREE (best option for regular monthly topups)
- E-wallet (GoPay, OVO, DANA, ShopeePay) — Rp 5,750 per topup
- Direct USD deposit — FREE (if you have a USD account)

The free bank transfer option is a key advantage over Pluang, which the user reported has become expensive and difficult for topups.

## User Reviews

GoTrade scores 4.7/5 on Google Play Store (8,000+ ratings) and 4.7/5 on App Store (2,400+ ratings). Users consistently praise:
- Clean, simple UI/UX that is beginner-friendly
- Low minimum investment ($1) enables easy dollar-cost averaging
- Free bank transfer topup
- Analyst signals and expert recommendations helpful for stock selection
- Price alerts and stop order features

Common complaints:
- Withdrawal fee $2 is high relative to small portfolio sizes
- Withdrawal process can be slow (several days)
- Only US stocks and ETFs (no crypto, no Indonesian market)
- Limited to 3 trades per 5-day window (not suitable for day trading)
- FX spread eats into small investments during closed market hours
- Customer service response times can be slow
