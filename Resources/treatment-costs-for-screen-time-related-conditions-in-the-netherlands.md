## Summary

Children exposed to high screen time may require treatment for associated conditions including ADHD, anxiety/depression, obesity, sleep disorders, and developmental delays. In the Netherlands, mental health care is delivered through the GGZ (Geestelijke Gezondheidszorg) system, with costs largely covered by the mandatory basisverzekering after the €385 annual deductible (eigen risico). Therapy sessions with a GZ-psycholoog or psychotherapist in contracted practices are reimbursed, while non-contracted or private practice sessions cost €90-€150 per 45-60 minute session.

For ADHD specifically, annual per-person healthcare costs in the Netherlands were estimated at €228 in direct medical costs (2005 values), or approximately €340-€380 when adjusted to 2026. This includes medication (stimulants such as methylphenidate), psychiatric consultations, and psychological therapy. Children with ADHD typically require ongoing monitoring, with 2-4 specialist visits per year and monthly medication reviews. The total direct healthcare cost per treated child ranges from €800-€1,500 per year depending on treatment intensity.

For childhood obesity, clinical interventions in the Netherlands include lifestyle counseling (€500-€1,000 per year), specialist pediatric care (€200-€400 per consultation), and in severe cases, bariatric surgery (€10,000-€15,000 one-time cost). For anxiety and depression related to excessive screen use, treatment through Basis GGZ involves 5-12 sessions over 3-6 months, with costs of €200-€600 per treatment episode after deductible. Specialist mental health care (Specialistische GGZ) for complex cases can cost €2,000-€5,000 per year. The total annual healthcare cost per child with screen-time-related conditions in the Netherlands is estimated at €1,200-€3,000, with significant variation by condition severity and comorbidity.

## Key Points

- **ADHD direct healthcare (Netherlands)**: €340-€380 per child per year (adjusted)
- **Total ADHD treatment cost**: €800-€1,500 per child per year
- **Mental health therapy (Basis GGZ)**: €200-€600 per treatment episode
- **Specialist mental health care**: €2,000-€5,000 per year
- **Childhood obesity clinical intervention**: €500-€2,000 per year
- **Bariatric surgery (severe cases)**: €10,000-€15,000 one-time
- **Dutch healthcare coverage**: basisverzekering covers contracted GGZ after €385 deductible
- **Private therapy (non-contracted)**: €90-€150 per session
- **Out-of-pocket with supplementary insurance**: €20-€60 per session
