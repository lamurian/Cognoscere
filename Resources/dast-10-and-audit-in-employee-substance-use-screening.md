## Summary

Two validated instruments are commonly used for substance use screening in employee health assessments: the Drug Abuse Screening Test (DAST-10) and the Alcohol Use Disorders Identification Test (AUDIT). Both are brief self-report tools suitable for occupational health settings.

The DAST-10 is a 10-item self-report instrument assessing drug use problems in the past 12 months. Items cover injection drug use, blackouts, drug use for non-medical reasons, inability to stop using, guilt feelings, withdrawal symptoms, overdose, concern from others, and use-related problems. Scores range from 0-10 with severity bands: 0 no problems, 1-2 low, 3-5 moderate, 6-8 substantial, 9-10 severe. Sensitivity 0.84 and specificity 0.85 at cutoff >=3 for identifying drug use disorders.

The AUDIT is a 10-item WHO-developed instrument measuring hazardous and harmful alcohol consumption. It covers frequency, quantity, dependence symptoms, and alcohol-related problems. Scores range 0-40: 0-7 low risk, 8-15 hazardous drinking, 16-19 harmful drinking, 20-40 possible dependence. Sensitivity 0.90 and specificity 0.88 for detecting hazardous/harmful drinking at cutoff >=8.

In recruitment contexts, substance use screening follows the same legal framework as mental health screening: it should occur only after a conditional job offer. Positive screens require follow-up clinical assessment, not automatic exclusion. Many candidates with substance use disorders respond well to treatment and can perform effectively in demanding roles with appropriate support.

## Key Points

- DAST-10: 10 items, 12-month recall, total 0-10; Se=0.84, Sp=0.85 at cutoff >=3
- AUDIT: 10 items (WHO), total 0-40; Se=0.90, Sp=0.88 at cutoff >=8
- DAST-10 severity: 0 none, 1-2 low, 3-5 moderate, 6-8 substantial, 9-10 severe
- AUDIT bands: 0-7 low risk, 8-15 hazardous, 16-19 harmful, 20-40 possible dependence
- Both administered post-offer only per ADA
- Positive screens require clinical follow-up, not exclusion decisions
- Important for safety-sensitive roles (transportation, heavy machinery, healthcare)
