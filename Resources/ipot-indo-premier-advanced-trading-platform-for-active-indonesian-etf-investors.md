## Summary

IPOT (Indo Premier Online Trading) is the digital trading platform of Indo Premier Sekuritas, one of Indonesia's oldest and most established securities companies, licensed since 1996 (OJK: KEP-11/PM/PPE/1996). IPOT is designed for active traders and experienced investors who need professional-grade tools including advanced charting, technical indicators, real-time data, and fast order execution. It is widely considered the go-to platform for serious traders in Indonesia.

## ETF Availability

IPOT offers full access to all ETFs listed on the Indonesia Stock Exchange (IDX), including ETF stocks and bonds. It provides standard order types (limit, market, stop loss, take profit) plus advanced features like RoboTrading for automated execution. IPOT focuses on Indonesian market instruments — for US ETFs, users need another platform.

## Fee Breakdown

**Stock/ETF transaction fees**:
- Buy: 0.19%
- Sell: 0.29%
- These fees include broker commission + BEI/KPEI/KSEI levy + PPN
- PPh final 0.1% applies on sell transactions

**Other services**:
- Margin trading: available for experienced investors
- Pre-opening order: available
- Mutual funds: no separate fee
- Corporate bond trading: available

**Topup**: No minimum deposit required; bank transfer to RDN is free.

## Topup Methods

IPOT supports standard bank transfer to the RDN (Rekening Dana Nasabah) at partner banks. There is no e-wallet topup option — its user base is more traditional investors who use bank transfers. Topup is free of charge.

## User Reviews

IPOT scores 4.3/5 on Bolsa24.net rating and is recommended for serious traders. Users consistently praise:
- Professional-grade charting tools and technical indicators
- Fast and reliable order execution with low latency
- Multi-platform access (mobile app, desktop app IPOT Pro, web)
- Comprehensive research reports from in-house analysts
- Real-time market data and corporate action calendar
- RoboTrading for automated strategies

Common complaints:
- Fee structure (0.19%/0.29%) is higher than Stockbit and Ajaib (both 0.15%/0.25%)
- Interface is dense and overwhelming for beginners
- No social/community features
- Desktop-heavy experience, mobile app less polished than competitors
- No US market access

## Key Facts

- Launch year: ~1996 (Indo Premier), IPOT platform later
- Users part of: Indo Premier Sekuritas (established brokerage)
- Buy fee: 0.19% | Sell fee: 0.29% (higher than Ajaib/Stockbit)
- ETF: All IDX-listed ETFs fully supported
- Margin trading: Available
- Advanced tools: Charting, RoboTrading, auto-orders
- Regulation: OJK (KEP-11/PM/PPE/1996)
- Minimum deposit: None
- Best for: Active traders and experienced investors needing professional-grade tools
