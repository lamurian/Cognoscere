## Summary

The European Skills, Competences, Qualifications and Occupations (ESCO) classification is a publicly accessible multilingual taxonomy developed by the European Commission to categorize occupations, skills, and qualifications across European labor markets. ESCO is particularly relevant for health worker specialization as it maps healthcare occupations to specific skills, competences, and qualification requirements.

ESCO's occupation pillar is built on the International Standard Classification of Occupations (ISCO-08), providing a hierarchical structure with 3,039 occupation concepts at Level 5 and below. Each occupation is mapped to exactly one ISCO-08 code and includes preferred terms, non-preferred terms, descriptions, scope notes, and regulatory information. Critically for health worker specialization, ESCO links each occupation to the knowledge, skills, and competences that experts consider relevant.

The classification covers healthcare professionals (ISCO Major Group 22: Health Professionals) and associate professionals (ISCO Sub-major Group 32: Health Associate Professionals), providing granular specialization archetypes including physicians, nurses, pharmacists, and allied health workers. ESCO is freely available in JSON, CSV, and PDF formats through the European Commission's website.

## Key Points

- **Structure**: Hierarchical classification based on ISCO-08 with 3,039 occupation concepts
- **Coverage**: 28 European languages, 13,485 skills, 4,022 qualifications, 3,039 occupations
- **Access**: Freely available at https://esco.ec.europa.eu (JSON, CSV, PDF)
- **Healthcare Groups**: ISCO 22 (Health Professionals), ISCO 32 (Health Associate Professionals)
- **Unique Feature**: Links occupations to skills, competences, and qualifications
- **Maintenance**: DG Employment, Social Affairs and Inclusion of the European Commission
- **Version**: ESCO v1.2.1 (Last update: October 2025)
