## Cost Analysis for $45 Monthly Investment

### GoTrade Indonesia — Best for monthly DCA
$45 buy per month:
- Trading fee: $0.10 (min, since 0.3% of $45 = $0.135 but min $0.10)
- JFX fee: $0.0225
- PPN: ~$0.0135
- **Total buy cost: ~$0.14 (0.3%)**
- Topup (bank transfer): FREE

If you sell after 6 months ($270):
- Sell fee: 0.3% × $270 = $0.81 (above $0.10 min)
- Add SEC/TAF/CAT: ~$0.03
- JFX + PPN on sell
- Withdrawal: $2
- **Total round-trip cost over 6 months: ~$3.50 on $270 = 1.3%**

**Verdict**: Best for monthly buy-and-hold DCA with free bank transfer topups.

### Pluang — Currently problematic per user report
The user reports Pluang has become expensive and difficult for topups. Previous research shows Pluang's fees are bundled (not transparent per-transaction), and the user's recent experience with increased topup fees makes it less suitable for small monthly amounts.

### Ajaib Alpha
$45 buy per month:
- Trading fee: 0.3% × $45 = $0.135
- No JFX fee or PPN on US trades
- **Total buy cost: ~$0.14**
- Topup (bank transfer): FREE
- Withdrawal: interbank fee (~Rp 2,000-6,500)

Requires Ajaib account. US ETF selection is more limited than GoTrade (no fractional shares for US ETFs on Ajaib Alpha — full shares only).

**Verdict**: Good if you already use Ajaib for IDX stocks, but limited by full-share pricing (some ETFs cost $400+ per share).

### eToro
- Min deposit: $50 (may be higher for Indonesia)
- $0 commission on US stocks/ETFs (spread-based, typically 0.09%-0.15%)
- Withdrawal fee: $5
- Inactivity fee after 12 months
- Not locally regulated in Indonesia (FCA/CySEC regulation only)

$45 buy per month:
- Spread cost: 0.09-0.15% × $45 = $0.04-$0.07
- **Total buy cost: ~$0.05-$0.07 (lower raw cost)**
- But min deposit $50 limits first purchase
- Withdrawal $5 is high for small accounts
- Currency conversion fees not transparent

**Verdict**: Lower per-trade cost but higher barriers ($50 min deposit, $5 withdrawal, no local regulation).

## Recommendation

**GoTrade Indonesia is the best option for monthly $45 US ETF investing from Indonesia** for the following reasons:

1. **Free bank transfer topup** — No topup fee, directly addressing the user's Pluang pain point
2. **Fractional shares from $1** — Buy any ETF with exactly $45 each month
3. **Local Bappebti regulation** — Legal protection via Valbury Asia Futures
4. **$1 minimum deposit** — Lowest barrier to start
5. **50+ US ETFs available** — Including VOO, SPY, QQQ for S&P 500 and Nasdaq exposure

The only significant cost to watch is the $2 withdrawal fee — minimize this by withdrawing only when the portfolio reaches meaningful size (every 6-12 months, or ideally hold long-term without withdrawing).

**Second best: Ajaib Alpha** if you want a single app for both IDX and US ETFs, but check whether US ETFs are available as fractional shares.

**eToro** offers lower per-trade fees but the $50 minimum deposit, $5 withdrawal fee, and lack of local regulation make it less suitable for Indonesian small investors.
