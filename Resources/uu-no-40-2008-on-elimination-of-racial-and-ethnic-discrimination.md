## Summary

UU No. 40/2008 is Indonesia's primary legislation against racial and ethnic discrimination, enacted to implement the International Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Racial Discrimination (ratified via UU No. 29/1999) and to fulfill constitutional guarantees of equality under Pancasila and the 1945 Constitution [@pemerintahrepublikindonesia2008].

The law defines discrimination based on race and ethnicity broadly as any distinction, exclusion, restriction, or preference based on race or ethnicity that results in the impairment of human rights and fundamental freedoms in civil, political, economic, social, and cultural fields (Article 1).

## Key Penalty Provisions

**Article 15** -- Any person who intentionally engages in discriminatory acts (differentiation, exclusion, restriction, or selection based on race/ethnicity, resulting in deprivation of human rights as defined in Article 4a) faces imprisonment of up to **1 year** and/or a fine of up to **Rp 100 million** (approximately USD 6,500).

**Article 16** -- Any person who intentionally shows hatred or animosity toward others based on racial/ethnic discrimination through written/graphic materials, public speeches, or symbols (Article 4b points 1-3) faces imprisonment of up to **5 years** and/or a fine of up to **Rp 500 million** (approximately USD 32,500).

**Article 17** -- Acts of violence including murder, assault, rape, robbery, or deprivation of liberty committed on the basis of racial/ethnic discrimination (Article 4b point 4) carry the base penalty for the underlying crime increased by **one-third**.

**Articles 19-21** -- Corporations can be held liable, with fines tripled compared to individual penalties, plus potential revocation of business licenses.

## Applicability

The law applies to "setiap orang" (every person), covering both Indonesian citizens and foreigners within Indonesian jurisdiction. Victims can file civil compensation claims (Articles 13-14), and Komnas HAM oversees enforcement (Article 8).

## Key Points

- Applies to all persons in Indonesian territory, including foreign tourists
- Direct discrimination and hate speech based on race/ethnicity are criminalized
- Maximum 5 years imprisonment for hate-based acts; 1 year for discriminatory treatment
- Corporate liability with 3x fines for organizational discrimination
- Civil remedies available via class action for compensation
- Komnas HAM monitors enforcement and can recommend action
