## Summary

Mental disorders are among the leading causes of non-fatal disease burden worldwide, and their prevalence has generally increased over recent decades. The Global Burden of Disease Study 2023 estimated that mental disorders affected approximately one in eight people globally, contributing over 400 million DALYs annually — roughly 16% of all global DALYs [@abdulla2025]. The GBD 2023 iteration assessed 12 mental disorders across 204 countries and territories, finding that the global prevalence and burden of mental disorders have risen from 1990 to 2023, with particularly steep increases in anxiety disorders and major depressive disorder [@gbdmentaldisorderscollaborators2025].

The trend is not uniform across all conditions or demographics. Depression and anxiety disorders show the most consistent upward trajectories, especially among adolescents and young adults aged 10-24. A GBD 2021 analysis of nine mental disorders in this age group found rising prevalence from 1990 to 2021, with projections suggesting continued increases to 2050. In low- and middle-income countries, the burden is growing faster due to population growth, urbanization, and conflict, yet treatment gaps remain extreme — up to 75% receive no care [@abdulla2025].

The COVID-19 pandemic accelerated these trends. A systematic review of anxiety disorders across 204 countries found that the pandemic significantly worsened global anxiety burden, with effects persisting into the post-pandemic period. The economic cost of mental ill-health is projected to exceed US$6 trillion by 2030, driven by lost productivity, healthcare expenditure, and social welfare costs.

## Key Points

- Mental disorders affect ~1 in 8 people globally; 16% of all DALYs
- Global prevalence has risen from 1990 to 2023, especially depression and anxiety
- Young people (10-24) show the steepest increases
- COVID-19 accelerated and may have permanently worsened trends
- 75% of people with mental disorders in LMICs receive no treatment
- Projected economic cost: >US$6 trillion by 2030
