## Summary

Proctoring introduces several threats to the validity of digital self-administered psychometric instruments. First, the surveillance context itself creates construct-irrelevant variance — test-taker responses may reflect anxiety about being watched rather than the intended psychological construct. Students report that being monitored leads to heightened anxiety, stress, and discomfort that negatively affects their performance [@coghlan2021; @heinrich2025]. For psychometric instruments measuring constructs like anxiety, depression, or stress, this artefactual elevation of distress confounds measurement.

Research on the equivalence of proctored versus unproctored internet-based testing for personality assessment has found that while some instruments show comparable psychometric properties across conditions, concerns about score inflation and cheating in unproctored settings remain [@coghlan2021]. However, the Heinrich review of 33 proctoring systems concluded that proctoring's effectiveness is questionable — one study comparing Proctorio against placebo found that online proctoring offered no significant improvement in assessment integrity [@heinrich2025; @linkletter2026].

For psychometric assessment specifically, the ecological validity of self-administered instruments depends partly on the respondent feeling comfortable enough to respond honestly. Self-report psychometric instruments were originally developed and validated in clinical or research settings with a human administrator present, not under continuous AI surveillance. Switching to proctored remote administration changes the testing context in ways that may affect the measurement properties established during validation studies, particularly for instruments validated without proctoring [@coghlan2021].

The tension between assessment security and measurement validity creates an ethical dilemma: proctoring is implemented to prevent cheating, but the very act of proctoring may alter the psychological state being measured.

## Key Points

- Proctoring creates construct-irrelevant variance through heightened test anxiety
- Psychometric instruments measuring anxiety, stress, or mood may be particularly affected
- Evidence suggests proctoring may not effectively improve assessment integrity
- Proctored remote administration deviates from original validation contexts
