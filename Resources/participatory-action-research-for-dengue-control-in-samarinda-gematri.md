## Summary

Participatory Action Research (PAR) for dengue control in Samarinda, East Kalimantan implemented the national G1R1J (One House One Jumantik) through community empowerment. Researchers facilitated workshops, socialization, FGDs, and mentoring. Community independently formed GEMATRI (Gerakan Emak-Emak Pembasmi Jentik Trisari) - mother-led larval eradication. Village issued decree delegating authority and budget for dengue activities from Health Office to sub-district. Puskesmas provided assistance and counseling. Larva-free index increased from 50.67% to 66.43%.

## Key Points

### Category: Collaborative (approaching co-created)
- **Researcher role**: Facilitate process; community identifies problems, designs solutions
- **Community agency**: Formed GEMATRI independently; initiated murals, training, policy advocacy
- **Policy impact**: Village decree on authority/budget delegation; Puskesmas role redefined as supporter

### Impacts
- Entomological improvement: Larva-free index +15.76 percentage points
- Community ownership: GEMATRI self-organized; murals as health communication
- Governance innovation: Village decree on dengue budget delegation (rare)
- Multi-stakeholder engagement: Cadres, village government, Puskesmas, mothers' groups

### Methodology
- **PAR cycles**: Workshops → Socialization → FGDs → Mentoring → Evaluation
- **Entomological surveys**: Pre- and post-intervention larval indices (House Index, Container Index, Breteau Index)
- **Community forums**: Identified local challenges, co-designed solutions
- **Cultural integration**: Murals, local language, mother-led groups (Emak-Emak)
- **Policy dialogue**: Engaged sub-district and Health Office for regulatory support

### Points of Improvement
1. **Scalability**: PAR intensive; requires skilled facilitators; not easily replicated at scale
2. **Sustainability**: GEMATRI dependent on motivated mothers; needs institutionalization
3. **Entomological validation**: Limited to larval indices; no dengue incidence data
4. **Gender dynamics**: Mother-focused; limited male/father engagement
5. **Digital integration**: Paper-based recording; no mobile reporting component
6. **Long-term follow-up**: No data on maintenance of larva-free index post-study
