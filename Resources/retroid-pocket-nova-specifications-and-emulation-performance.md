## Summary

The Retroid Pocket Nova is a 4.5-inch AMOLED Android handheld released in July 2026, priced at $229-$274. It uses a Qualcomm QCS8550 processor — effectively a Snapdragon 8 Gen 2-class chip without cellular components — paired with an Adreno 740 GPU. The device targets retro emulation with a 4:3 display that matches classic console aspect ratios.

The QCS8550 features one prime core at 3.2GHz, four performance cores at 2.8GHz, and three efficiency cores at 2.0GHz. RAM options are 8GB or 12GB LPDDR5X, with 128GB UFS 3.1 storage. The 4.5-inch AMOLED display runs at 1280x960 (4:3) with 120Hz refresh and 500 nits brightness. Battery is 5,000mAh with 27W fast charging.

Benchmark scores from independent reviews place the Nova at competitive parity with other Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 handhelds. AnTuTu scores around 1,497,067, Geekbench 6 reaches 2,053 single-core and 5,564 multi-core, and 3DMark Wildlife Extreme scores 3,732. The Nova leads competitors in Geekbench and 3DMark tests despite trailing slightly in AnTuTu [@davec2026].

Emulation performance is strong for systems up to PlayStation 2. PS2 games run comfortably at upscaled resolutions (3-4x native) with filters using NetherSX2 Turnip. Dreamcast (Flycast), GameCube (Dolphin), and PS1 (DuckStation) all perform well with visual enhancements. The 4:3 display is ideal for pre-widescreen-era consoles but introduces borders on newer 16:9 systems. Battery life ranges from 3-5 hours under heavy emulation to approximately 8 hours under lighter use [@retrododo2026; @davec2026].

## Key Points

- Qualcomm QCS8550 (Snapdragon 8 Gen 2-class) with Adreno 740 GPU
- 4.5-inch AMOLED, 1280x960, 4:3, 120Hz, 500 nits
- 8GB/12GB LPDDR5X RAM, 128GB UFS 3.1 storage
- PS2 emulation at 3-4x upscale; strong Dreamcast, GameCube, PS1 performance
- 5,000mAh battery, 3-5 hours heavy use, 27W fast charging
- Android 13, Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 5.3
- Hall-effect joysticks, analog triggers, USB-C video out
