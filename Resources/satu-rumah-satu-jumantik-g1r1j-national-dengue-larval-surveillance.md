## Summary

Satu Rumah Satu Jumantik (One House One Dengue Monitor / G1R1J) is a national dengue control movement where households appoint a family member (Jumantik) to inspect water containers for Aedes larvae weekly. Kader coordinate, compile biweekly reports, and submit to Puskesmas. Implemented since 2010s, scaled to 65M target households. Participatory action research in Samarinda (2024) evolved this into GEMATRI (Gerakan Emak-Emak Pembasmi Jentik Trisari) - mother-led larval eradication with murals, village-decreed authority, and budget delegation.

## Key Points

### Category: Contributory → Collaborative (in PAR sites)
- **Standard G1R1J**: Households follow MoH protocol; data flows upward (contributory)
- **Samarinda PAR**: Community formed GEMATRI, initiated murals, secured village decree and budget (collaborative)

### Impacts
- Larva-free index increased from 50.67% to 66.43% in Samarinda PAR site
- 33,392 voluntary dengue monitors in Jakarta across 30,000 communities (2016)
- National target: 65M households; actual reach varies by region
- Integrated with Smart City surveillance in Jakarta

### Methodology
- **Household level**: Family member checks containers weekly (3M: drain, cover, recycle)
- **Kader level**: Biweekly household visits, record larval indices on paper, report to Puskesmas
- **Puskesmas level**: Compile, analyze, target fogging/larviciding (Abate)
- **Digital**: Some areas pilot mobile reporting; most remain paper-based
- **PAR enhancement (Samarinda)**: Workshops, FGDs, mentoring; entomological surveys pre/post

### Points of Improvement
1. **Sustainability**: Volunteer fatigue; reliant on PKK women's groups
2. **Data quality**: Paper-based, inconsistent larval identification skills
3. **Digital integration**: No national app for real-time larval indices
4. **Equity**: Urban areas better covered; remote villages lack kader capacity
5. **Incentives**: Jumantik unpaid; kader incentives minimal and inconsistent
6. **Entomological validation**: Limited lab confirmation of larval indices
