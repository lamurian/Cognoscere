## Summary

The Cina Benteng maintain a unique hybrid culture blending Chinese, Sundanese, and Betawi traditions. Despite having abandoned the Chinese language generations ago, they preserve distinctive customs that set them apart from other Chinese Indonesian communities. Their cultural practices reflect six centuries of intermarriage and daily interaction with local populations.

**Weddings** are the most distinctive tradition: Cina Benteng couples still use Manchu dynasty (Qing) style ceremonies, including Manchu robes and hats. This makes them the only Chinese community in Indonesia with Manchu blood and the only one preserving Qing-era wedding customs after the dynasty fell in 1912. The Teh Pai (tea ceremony) is a ritual communication process passed through generations.

**Religious practices** blend Confucianism, Taoism, Buddhism, and ancestor worship under the Cio Tao tradition. The Boen Tek Bio temple (founded 1684) hosts the Gotong Toapekong procession every 12 years, a major community event centered on Dewi Kwan Im Hud Couw (Goddess of Mercy). The community believes this deity protected the temple from flooding during the 1883 Krakatau eruption and from two unexploded Japanese bombs in 1942.

**Culinary traditions** include kecap (sweet soy sauce) production — Tangerang's sugar cane plantations transformed salty Chinese soy sauce into the sweet variant (kecap manis) now used across Indonesia. The Teng Giok Seng brand has operated since 1882. Other traditions include Cokek dance (paired male-female dance with gambang kromong music), Peh-Cun (Dragon Boat Festival) ceremonies, and Cap Go Meh celebrations.

## Key Points

- **Weddings**: Manchu dynasty style — robes, hats, Teh Pai tea ceremony
- **Religion**: Cio Tao tradition blending Confucianism, Taoism, Buddhism, ancestor worship
- **Temples**: Boen Tek Bio (1684), Gotong Toapekong every 12 years
- **Cuisine**: Kecap manis originated from Chinese soy sauce + Tangerang sugar cane
- **Music/Dance**: Cokek dance, gambang kromong ensemble
- **Festivals**: Peh-Cun (Dragon Boat), Cap Go Meh, Ceng Beng (Qingming)
