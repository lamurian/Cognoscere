## Summary

Childhood obesity carries substantial lifetime economic costs that extend far beyond direct healthcare expenditures. The JANPA (Joint Action on Nutrition and Physical Activity) EU-funded study estimated the lifetime cost per child with overweight/obesity at €16,036 in the Republic of Ireland and €22,647 in Northern Ireland (2015 values). These costs include direct healthcare costs, lifetime income loss ('income penalty'), and productivity losses from premature death and absenteeism. In the Republic of Ireland, childhood obesity/overweight accounted for 1.6% of GDP, with an estimated 55,000 of today's children expected to die prematurely as a result.

Direct healthcare costs alone account for approximately 20-25% of the total lifetime burden. In the Republic of Ireland, total lifetime direct healthcare costs were estimated at €944.7 million for the cohort, or approximately €3,370 per person. The JANPA model found that males incur higher total lifetime costs (€21,115 per person) than females (€11,694 per person), primarily driven by higher productivity losses from premature mortality. A 5% reduction in mean childhood BMI would yield lifetime savings of over €1 billion in Ireland alone.

For the Netherlands, the OECD reports that obesity-related healthcare costs account for approximately 2-3% of total healthcare expenditure. Using the JANPA methodology and adjusting for PPP (Netherlands PPP factor ~0.95 relative to Ireland), the estimated lifetime cost per child with overweight/obesity in the Netherlands would be approximately €15,200-€21,500. With a childhood overweight/obesity prevalence of approximately 15-20% in the Netherlands (2023 data), the annualized per-child cost would be approximately €600-€850 per year across the affected childhood population. Direct treatment costs for childhood obesity in clinical settings range from €500-€2,000 per child per year depending on severity and intervention type.

## Key Points

- **Lifetime cost per child (Ireland)**: €16,036 (2015 values, JANPA study)
- **Lifetime cost per child (Netherlands estimate, PPP-adjusted)**: €15,200-€21,500
- **Direct healthcare component**: ~20-25% of total lifetime cost
- **Annual direct treatment costs (Netherlands)**: €500-€2,000 per child per year
- **Largest cost component**: productivity losses from premature mortality (60-63% of total)
- **Gender difference**: males incur ~80% higher lifetime costs than females
- **5% BMI reduction savings**: >€1 billion lifetime per country cohort
- **Netherlands prevalence**: ~15-20% of children overweight/obese
