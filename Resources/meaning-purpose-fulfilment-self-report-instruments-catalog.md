## Summary

Meaning, purpose, and fulfilment instruments measure eudaimonic well-being, purpose in life, personal growth, self-acceptance, and engagement. This catalog documents 12 widely validated instruments spanning existential, humanistic, and positive psychology traditions. These instruments map to ICF Personal Factors and complement hedonic well-being measures.

## Instrument Catalog

**1. MLQ (Meaning in Life Questionnaire)**
Authors: Steger et al., 2006. 10 items across 2 subscales: Presence of Meaning (5 items) and Search for Meaning (5 items). 7-point Likert (1-7). Mean score per subscale. alpha=0.82-0.92. Translated and validated in 30+ languages. Manual: https://meaninginlife.com/

**2. Ryff's Psychological Well-Being Scales (PWB-42)**
Authors: Ryff, 1989. 42 items across 6 subscales (7 items each): Self-Acceptance, Positive Relations with Others, Autonomy, Environmental Mastery, Purpose in Life, Personal Growth. 6-point Likert (1-6). Subscale scores. alpha=0.69-0.92. Manual: https://psycnet.apa.org/purchase/1990-29157-001

**3. Ryff's PWB Scales Short Form (PWB-18)**
Authors: Ryff & Keyes, 1995. 18 items (3 per subscale). Same 6 dimensions. 6-point Likert. Subscale scores. alpha=0.63-0.84. Suitable for large surveys. Manual: freely available in original publication.

**4. SWLS (Satisfaction with Life Scale)**
Authors: Diener et al., 1985. 5 items, 7-point Likert (1-7). Total 5-35. Cutoffs: <=9 extremely dissatisfied, 10-14 dissatisfied, 15-19 slightly dissatisfied, 20-24 slightly satisfied, 25-29 satisfied, 30-35 highly satisfied. alpha=0.87. Widely used hedonic well-being measure. Manual: freely available.

**5. Purpose in Life Test (PIL)**
Authors: Crumbaugh & Maholick, 1964. 20 items, 7-point semantic differential. Total 20-140. Cutoff <=92 indicates absence of purpose. alpha=0.82-0.90. Existential psychology instrument. Manual: https://www.pearsonclinical.com/

**6. Flourishing Scale**
Authors: Diener et al., 2010. 8 items, 7-point Likert (1-7). Total 8-56. Higher = greater flourishing. Covers purpose, relationships, engagement, accomplishment, self-worth, optimism, belonging. alpha=0.87. Manual: freely available.

**7. PERMA Profiler**
Authors: Butler & Kern, 2016. 23 items across 5 subscales: Positive Emotion, Engagement, Relationships, Meaning, Accomplishment (4 items each) plus 3 items on negative emotion and 1 on overall well-being. 0-10 scale. Subscale means. alpha=0.79-0.90. Manual: https://www.perma profiler.org/

**8. PROMIS Meaning and Purpose**
Authors: Furr et al., 2021. 7-item short form or CAT. T-score (mean=50, SD=10). Measures sense of meaning, direction, and significance. alpha=0.93. Part of PROMIS suite. Manual: https://www.healthmeasures.net/explore-measurement-systems/promis

**9. Authentic Happiness Inventory (AHI)**
Authors: Seligman et al., 2005. 24 items across 6 PERMA domains, 5-point Likert. Total score or domain scores. alpha=0.85-0.92. Maps to Seligman's flourishing model. Manual: https://www.authentichappiness.sas.upenn.edu/

**10. Values in Action Inventory of Character Strengths (VIA-IS)**
Authors: Peterson et al., 2005. 240 items (10 per strength) assessing 24 character strengths. 5-point scale. Individual strength profiles. alpha=0.70-0.93 across strengths. Manual: https://www.viacharacter.org/

**11. Personal Growth Initiative Scale (PGIS-II)**
Authors: Robitschek, 1998. 9 items, 6-point Likert. Total score. alpha=0.90-0.95. Measures active engagement in personal growth. Manual: freely available.

**12. Eudaimonic Well-Being Scale (EWBS)**
Authors: Waterman et al., 2010. 21 items across 2 subscales: Authentic Personal Development (12 items) and Pleasantness of Living (9 items). 5-point Likert. Subscale and total scores. alpha=0.80-0.87. Distinguishes eudaimonic from hedonic well-being. Manual: freely available.

## Key Points

- MLQ and Purpose in Life Test measure existential meaning directly; Ryff PWB captures broader eudaimonic functioning
- SWLS and Flourishing Scale bridge hedonic and eudaimonic traditions
- PERMA Profiler operationalizes Seligman's complete well-being model across 5 domains
- PROMIS Meaning and Purpose enables integration with physical health measurement
- VIA-IS uniquely identifies character strengths as pathways to fulfilment
- Ryff PWB 42-item and 18-item versions offer choice between depth and brevity
