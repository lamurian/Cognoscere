## Summary

Ajaib is an Indonesian multi-asset investment platform launched in 2018 by PT Ajaib Sekuritas Asia (kode broker XC). It targets millennial and Gen Z retail investors with a modern, user-friendly interface. Ajaib was founded by Y Combinator-backed founders and has raised $243M across 3 funding rounds, reaching a $1B valuation. As of mid-2023, Ajaib recorded 3 million retail investors across its stock trading and crypto products, and is the third-largest brokerage in Indonesia by transaction volume. In 2025, Ajaib posted IDR 474 billion net revenue (+152% YoY) and crossed into profitability for the first time.

## ETF Availability and Products

Ajaib offers Indonesian ETFs and US ETFs (via Ajaib Alpha). Beyond ETFs, it provides Indonesian stocks, US stocks, mutual funds, crypto (spot and futures), and options trading. It is a true all-in-one platform regulated by OJK (securities) and Bappebti (crypto).

## Fee Breakdown

**Stock/ETF transaction fees** (under Rp 150M per transaction):
- Buy: 0.1513% (0.15% broker + 0.0433% levy + PPN 12%)
- Sell: 0.2513% (0.25% broker + 0.0433% levy + PPh final 0.1% + PPN 12%)
- Market Order: additional 0.1%
- Forced sell: additional 0.25%

**Tiered pricing** (per transaction value):
- Rp 150M-Rp 1.5B: 0.1412% buy / 0.2412% sell
- Over Rp 1.5B: 0.1311% buy / 0.2311% sell

**Other fees**:
- Mutual funds: free buy/sell
- Margin trading: 0.05% per day on limit used
- Day Trade (leverage up to 20x): 0.2% per day
- Crypto: Maker 0.111%/Taker 0.222% + SRO fees
- Topup/transfer: free (no topup fee)

## Topup Methods

Ajaib supports bank transfer topups directly to the RDN (Rekening Dana Nasabah). No additional topup fee is charged. Withdrawal from RDN to external bank accounts incurs a small interbank transfer fee (Rp 2,000 as tested).

## User Reviews

Ajaib scores 4.3/5 on Google Play Store and 4.1/5 on Apple App Store (as of May 2026). Users praise the modern UI, ease of registration (100% online, RDN activates within hours), and comprehensive product range. Common complaints include: the app can be heavy/performance-intensive, some users report slow customer support, and charting tools are basic compared to professional platforms. The Rankia editorial team (April 2026) tested Ajaib and found that during high market volatility, order execution experienced delays of up to 17 minutes, though regular trading days executed instantly. Withdrawal from RDN Permata to BCA took ~44 minutes.
