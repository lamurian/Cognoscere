## Summary

In clinical practice and healthcare epidemiology, a chief complaint represents the primary symptom, functional impairment, or self-reported distress that prompts an individual to seek medical or health services [@stsauver2013]. Unlike formal diagnostic codes established after clinical evaluation, chief complaints reflect the patient's immediate reason for visit across primary care clinics, urgent care centers, and emergency departments [@cdcnationalcenterforhealthstatistics2022].

Systematic analyses of population-based healthcare encounters and ambulatory medical care surveys identify five main categories of health and wellbeing chief complaints [@stsauver2013; @vandenberg2018]:

1. **Acute and Chronic Pain Symptoms**: Pain constitutes the single most prevalent chief complaint, accounting for over 40% of all ambulatory and emergency visits. Top complaints include musculoskeletal pain (e.g., low back pain, joint stiffness), chest pain (requiring cardiac triage), abdominal pain, and severe headache [@moore2021].
2. **Respiratory Symptoms and Airway Distress**: Acute respiratory complaints represent the highest volume non-pain reason for visit. Symptoms include acute cough, dyspnea (shortness of breath), sore throat, and nasal congestion, frequently driven by upper and lower respiratory infections or asthma exacerbations [@vandenberg2018].
3. **Mental Health and Psychological Distress Symptoms**: Mental wellbeing complaints are among the fastest-growing reasons for seeking care. Patients commonly present with acute anxiety, persistent depressive symptoms, overwhelming fatigue, and severe insomnia or sleep disturbances [@stsauver2013].
4. **Gastrointestinal Disturbances**: Non-pain gastrointestinal complaints rank prominently in both urgent care and emergency presentations. Key complaints include nausea, vomiting, acute diarrhea, acid reflux, and changes in bowel habits [@moore2021].
5. **General Systemic and Constitutional Symptoms**: Constitutional symptoms reflect systemic physiological disruptions. Common chief complaints in this category include fever, generalized weakness, unexplained fatigue, dizziness or vertigo, and cutaneous eruptions (skin rashes or soft tissue swellings) [@cdcnationalcenterforhealthstatistics2022].

Understanding these five prevalent chief complaint categories enables healthcare providers and health system planners to optimize triage protocols, allocate diagnostic resources efficiently, and integrate physical and mental health services in primary care [@stsauver2013].

## Key Points

- **Definition**: Chief complaints are self-reported symptoms/reasons for visit, distinct from physician-assigned diagnoses [@stsauver2013].
- **Pain**: Musculoskeletal, abdominal, chest, and head pain account for the largest overall proportion of care-seeking events [@moore2021].
- **Respiratory Distress**: Cough, fever, and dyspnea lead acute primary and emergency visits [@vandenberg2018].
- **Psychological Wellbeing**: Anxiety, depression, insomnia, and fatigue drive a significant volume of outpatient encounters [@stsauver2013].
- **Systemic & GI Disruptions**: Nausea, diarrhea, fever, and dizziness complete the top five categories [@cdcnationalcenterforhealthstatistics2022].
