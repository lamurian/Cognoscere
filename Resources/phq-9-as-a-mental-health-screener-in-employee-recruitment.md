## Summary

The Patient Health Questionnaire-9 (PHQ-9) is a 9-item self-report depression screening instrument widely used in clinical and occupational settings. Each item maps directly to DSM-5 major depressive disorder criteria, scored 0-3 over a two-week recall period (total range 0-27). Severity thresholds: 5-9 minimal, 10-14 moderate, 15-19 moderately severe, 20-27 severe.

The original validation by Kroenke et al. (2001) in 6,000 primary care patients found sensitivity 0.88 and specificity 0.88 at cutoff >=10. A 2019 IPD meta-analysis across 29 studies confirmed sensitivity 0.88 and specificity 0.85 at the same threshold. The PHQ-9 is the most widely validated brief depression screener globally.

In employee recruitment contexts, the PHQ-9 is typically administered as part of a post-offer pre-placement health assessment rather than during initial screening, due to ADA restrictions on pre-offer medical inquiries. The tool helps occupational health professionals identify candidates who may benefit from workplace accommodations or early intervention support. A positive screen (score >=10) should trigger a clinical follow-up interview, not serve as a standalone basis for hiring decisions.

## Key Points

- 9 items aligned with DSM-5 MDD criteria; 0-3 Likert scale; 2-week recall; total 0-27
- Validation: Se=0.88, Sp=0.88 at cutoff >=10 (Kroenke 2001); confirmed in IPD meta-analysis (Levis 2019)
- In recruitment: appropriate only after conditional job offer (post-offer, pre-placement)
- Positive screen (>=10) requires clinical follow-up interview, not standalone decision-making
- Must be administered uniformly to all candidates in the same job category per ADA
- Results must be kept in confidential medical files separate from personnel records
