## Summary

The Global South's alignment patterns are reshaping great-power competition. The CFR report identifies six "global swing states" — Brazil, India, Indonesia, Saudi Arabia, South Africa, and Turkey — as middle powers with sufficient geopolitical weight to sway the future direction of the international order (@goldberg2024). Their policy preferences could determine whether the post-Western world tilts toward authoritarian revisionism or liberal institutional reform.

China's strategy in the Global South operates through economic engagement, infrastructure investment, and personnel placement in multilateral institutions. Through the AIIB and Islamic Development Bank, China provides alternatives to Western-led financial institutions with more favorable terms and fewer governance conditions. The Institute for Economics and Peace documents how BRICS expansion and SCO development create parallel institutional architectures that challenge Western institutional dominance (@instituteforeconomicsandpeace2025).

The non-aligned pattern is evident in voting behavior: 17 African nations abstained on the 2022 Ukraine resolution, reflecting diplomatic balancing rather than automatic alignment with either bloc. Turkey exemplifies strategic ambiguity — courting Russia and China while maintaining NATO membership. The CSIS analysis argues these nations are not passively non-aligned but actively asserting independent foreign policies shaped by specific national interests rather than ideological blocs (@runde2024).

RAND research on secondary theaters finds China and Russia competing for influence in Latin America, Africa, and the Middle East through arms sales, infrastructure investment, and diplomatic support. The key variable is whether these swing states develop closer security partnerships with China or Russia that would shift regional military balances.

## Key Points

- Six swing states identified: Brazil, India, Indonesia, Saudi Arabia, South Africa, Turkey
- BRICS expansion and SCO create parallel institutional architectures
- 17 African nations abstained on Ukraine vote — diplomatic balancing, not bloc alignment
- China uses AIIB, IsDB for alternative financial architecture with fewer conditions
- Key variable: swing states' security partnerships with China/Russia would shift military balances
