## Summary

Game performance on the Retroid Pocket Nova via GameNative depends on each game's system requirements and optimization. The Nova's Snapdragon 8 Gen 2-class QCS8550 with Adreno 740 GPU provides adequate power for lighter PC titles but struggles with demanding 3D games. GameNative 1.2's automatic power tuning helps maintain stable frame rates on supported hardware including the Nova.

**Terraria** is a 2D sandbox with modest system requirements. On Snapdragon 8 Gen 2-class devices, it runs well through GameNative with 60+ FPS achievable. The game's lightweight nature means the Nova handles it without significant issues. Touch controls can be configured through GameNative's on-screen editor, and the 4:3 display provides adequate screen space for the 2D viewport.

**Stardew Valley** is a 2D farming simulation also with modest requirements. Reports from the GameNative community indicate locked 60fps performance on Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 devices with Box64 Preset set to Compatibility. On Mali GPU devices, performance reaches 60-70 FPS with occasional dips to the 50s at lowest settings. The Nova's Adreno 740 should match or exceed this. Stardew Valley has a native Android port, which offers an alternative with potentially better optimization, though the Steam version via GameNative preserves PC save compatibility.

**Valheim** is a demanding 3D survival game that requires significantly more GPU and CPU resources than the other two titles. Valheim is not confirmed as fully playable on Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 devices through GameNative based on available community reports. The game's 3D rendering, procedural world generation, and particle effects stress mobile GPUs significantly. Users may need to reduce resolution and graphical settings substantially, and performance may remain inconsistent. The 4:3 display also introduces letterboxing since Valheim is designed for widescreen. For reliable Valheim play, a dedicated handheld PC (Steam Deck, ROG Ally) remains the better choice.

## Key Points

- **Terraria**: Runs well at 60+ FPS; lightweight 2D game, no significant issues on Nova
- **Stardew Valley**: 60 FPS locked on Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 via GameNative; native Android port also available
- **Valheim**: Demanding 3D game; not confirmed fully playable on SD8G2 via GameNative; may need significant settings reduction
- GameNative 1.2 auto-tuning helps maintain stable FPS on Nova hardware
- 4:3 display suits Terraria and Stardew Valley; Valheim requires widescreen adaptation
- Community configs auto-applied for known compatible games
