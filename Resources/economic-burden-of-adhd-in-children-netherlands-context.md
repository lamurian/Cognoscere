## Summary

ADHD is one of the most costly neurodevelopmental disorders in childhood, with substantial direct healthcare, education, and societal costs. In the Netherlands, the landmark study by Hakkaart-van Roijen et al. @hakkaartvanroijen2007 estimated that the annual societal cost per child with ADHD was approximately €1,866 (2005 euros), with healthcare costs accounting for roughly €228 per child and productivity losses for parents/caregivers constituting the largest component. When adjusted for inflation to 2026 euros, this translates to approximately €2,800-€3,000 per child per year.

A 2022 US study by Schein et al. @schein2022 found that the total annual societal excess costs associated with ADHD were $6,799 per child (aged 5-11) and $8,349 per adolescent (aged 12-17) in the US. Education costs contributed approximately 50-60% of the total burden, followed by direct healthcare costs (26-29%) and caregiving costs (11-14%). Applying PPP conversion (Netherlands PPP factor ~0.78 relative to US), these figures translate to approximately €5,300-€6,500 per child in Netherlands-equivalent purchasing power.

A 2024 systematic review and meta-analysis by Dodds et al. @dodds2024 found that the weighted mean annual direct medical cost was $5,319 for children with ADHD compared to $1,152 for matched counterparts, yielding an excess of $4,167 per child. The global economic burden review by Chhibber et al. @chhibber2021 reported annual total costs ranging from $831 to $20,538 per person across countries. The wide range reflects differences in healthcare systems, diagnostic practices, and cost methodologies. For the Netherlands specifically, direct healthcare costs are covered by the mandatory basisverzekering after the eigen risico (€385 deductible), with additional costs from special education and parental productivity losses.

## Key Points

- **Netherlands baseline (2005)**: ~€1,866 per child per year societal cost (@hakkaartvanroijen2007)
- **Netherlands adjusted (2026)**: ~€2,800-€3,000 per child per year (inflation-adjusted)
- **US equivalent (PPP-adjusted)**: ~€5,300-€6,500 per child per year
- **Direct healthcare excess**: ~$4,167 per child per year (global meta-analysis)
- **Education costs**: largest single component (50-60% of total burden)
- **Caregiving costs**: 11-14% of total burden (parental productivity loss)
- **Prevalence in Netherlands**: ~5% of children (2-4% global range)
- **Total national burden**: estimated €280-€450 million annually (extrapolated from prevalence and per-child costs)
