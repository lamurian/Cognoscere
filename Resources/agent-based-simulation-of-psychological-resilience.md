## Summary
This agent-based simulation replicates psychological resilience dynamics in a computational framework, using the Mesa platform. It integrates mechanism groups for stress response and adaptation, modeling resilience as an emergent property in complex adaptive systems.

The model emphasizes individual variation and dynamic interactions between individuals and their environment. It allows for the exploration of diverse scenarios and the study of how resilience emerges from the interaction of multiple factors, including stress exposure, adaptive capacity, and environmental resources.

## Key Points
- Uses the Mesa agent-based modeling framework.
- Models resilience as an emergent property.
- Integrates stress response and adaptation mechanisms.
- Captures individual variation and dynamic interactions.
- Validation remains challenging due to lack of standardized benchmarks; parameter calibration can be complex and sensitive.
