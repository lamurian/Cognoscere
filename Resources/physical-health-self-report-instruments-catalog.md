## Summary

Physical health self-report instruments measure bodily functioning, symptoms, pain, fatigue, sleep, and physical capacity. This catalog documents 12 widely validated instruments across clinical, research, and population health settings. These instruments map primarily to ICF Body Functions and partially to Activities and Participation.

## Instrument Catalog

**1. SF-36 (Short Form-36 Health Survey)**
Authors: Ware & Sherbourne, 1992. 36 items across 8 domains: Physical Functioning, Role Physical, Bodily Pain, General Health, Vitality, Social Functioning, Role Emotional, Mental Health. Scores 0-100 per domain; Physical Component Summary (PCS) and Mental Component Summary (MCS) normed to mean=50, SD=10. Internal consistency: alpha 0.73-0.94 across domains. Manual: https://www.optum.com/quality-and-advancement/health-surveys

**2. EQ-5D-5L**
Authors: EuroQol Group, 1990; updated 2011. 5 dimensions (Mobility, Self-Care, Usual Activities, Pain/Discomfort, Anxiety/Depression) each with 5 levels. Generates utility index (0=dead to 1=full health) using country-specific value sets. Visual Analogue Scale (VAS) 0-100 for overall health. Responsive to change across 200+ conditions. Manual: https://euroqol.org/eq-5d-instruments

**3. PROMIS Physical Function (PF)**
Authors: Cella et al., 2007. Item banks of 12-127 items using Computer Adaptive Testing (CAT) or short forms (4-8 items). T-score metric (mean=50, SD=10). Covers ability to perform tasks, move, and accomplish physical activities. ICC 0.92-0.96 for CAT. Manual: https://www.healthmeasures.net/explore-measurement-systems/promis

**4. PROMIS Pain Interference**
Authors: Amtmann et al., 2010. 7-item short form or CAT. T-score (mean=50, SD=10). Measures extent pain interferes with social, cognitive, and physical activities. alpha=0.95 for short form. Manual: https://www.healthmeasures.net/explore-measurement-systems/promis

**5. PROMIS Fatigue**
Authors: Lai et al., 2003. 13-item short form or CAT. T-score (mean=50, SD=10). Measures frequency, severity, and impact of fatigue. alpha=0.95. Manual: https://www.healthmeasures.net/explore-measurement-systems/promis

**6. PROMIS Sleep Disturbance**
Authors: Buysse et al., 2010. 8-item short form or CAT. T-score (mean=50, SD=10). Measures sleep quality, latency, and disturbance. alpha=0.90. Manual: https://www.healthmeasures.net/explore-measurement-systems/promis

**7. Nottingham Health Profile (NHP)**
Authors: Hunt et al., 1980. 38 items across 6 sections: Energy, Pain, Emotional Reactions, Sleep, Physical Mobility, Social Isolation. Each section scored 0-100. Validated across 30+ countries. Used for population health surveys. Manual: https://www.nottingham.ac.uk/research/groups/medical-psychology-and-medical-sociology

**8. WHOQOL-BREF (Physical Domain)**
Authors: WHOQOL Group, 1998. 26 items across 4 domains; Physical Domain has 7 items. Scores 0-100 (transformed). Covers activity, energy, sleep, mobility, pain, work capacity. alpha=0.80. Cross-culturally validated in 50+ countries. Manual: https://www.who.int/teams/mental-health-and-substance-use/research-quality-of-life/whoqol

**9. PROMIS Global Health**
Authors: Hays et al., 2009. 10 items measuring global physical and mental health. Physical Health scale (4 items) and Mental Health scale (6 items). T-scores (mean=50, SD=10). alpha=0.81-0.89. Manual: https://www.healthmeasures.net/explore-measurement-systems/promis

**10. Brief Pain Inventory (BPI)**
Authors: Cleeland & Ryan, 1994. 15 items: 4 pain severity items (0-10 NRS), 7 pain interference items (0-10 NRS), plus patient description. Severity and interference subscales. alpha=0.85-0.87. Validated in 10+ languages. Manual: https://www.mdanderson.org/departments/pain-research/outcomes.html

**11. DASH (Disabilities of the Arm, Shoulder and Hand)**
Authors: Hudak et al., 1996. 30 items on 5-point Likert (1-5). Score: sum/30*25-25 (0-100, higher=worse). Covers symptoms and functional limitation of upper extremity. alpha=0.96. Manual: https://dash.iwh.on.ca/

**12. Roland-Morris Disability Questionnaire**
Authors: Roland & Morris, 1983. 24 items describing physical disability due to low back pain (yes/no). Score: 0-24. Cutoff >=14 indicates significant disability. alpha=0.89-0.93. Manual: freely available in original publication.

## Key Points

- Generic instruments (SF-36, EQ-5D, PROMIS) enable cross-population comparison; condition-specific instruments (BPI, DASH) capture targeted domains
- PROMIS uses IRT-based scoring with CAT for precision and efficiency
- EQ-5D is the gold standard for health economic evaluation (QALY)
- SF-36 and NHP remain widely used in population health surveys
- Physical health instruments map to ICF Body Functions chapters
