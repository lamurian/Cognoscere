## Summary

UU No. 39/1999 on Human Rights establishes the foundational legal framework for human rights protection in Indonesia, predating and complementing the more specific UU No. 40/2008 on racial/ethnic discrimination [@pemerintahrepublikindonesia1999].

The law defines discrimination broadly in Article 1 as "any restriction, harassment, or exclusion whether direct or indirect based on distinctions of religion, ethnicity, race, group, social status, economic status, gender, language, political belief, resulting in the reduction, deviation, or elimination of the recognition, implementation, or use of human rights and fundamental freedoms."

## Key Non-Discrimination Provisions

**Article 3** -- Every person is entitled to recognition, guarantee, protection, and fair legal treatment as well as certainty of law and equal treatment before the law. Every person is entitled to protection of human rights and fundamental freedoms, without discrimination.

**Article 5** -- Every person is recognized as an individual entitled to demand and receive equal treatment and protection in accordance with their human dignity before the law.

**Article 26** -- Every person is free to choose their citizenship and without discrimination is entitled to enjoy rights derived from and inherent in that citizenship.

**Article 69** -- Every person is obliged to respect the human rights of others, morality, ethics, and order of social, national, and state life.

## Significance for Anti-Discrimination

UU 39/1999 establishes the constitutional and legal basis that all forms of discrimination are violations of fundamental human rights. It provides the underlying framework that UU 40/2008 later operationalizes with specific criminal penalties. The law also establishes Komnas HAM (National Human Rights Commission) as the monitoring body.

## Key Points

- Defines discrimination broadly across multiple grounds (religion, ethnicity, race, group, gender, etc.)
- Establishes equal treatment before the law as a fundamental right
- Obligates every person to respect others' human rights
- Provides the legal foundation for specialized anti-discrimination legislation
- Enabled the establishment of Komnas HAM as enforcement body
