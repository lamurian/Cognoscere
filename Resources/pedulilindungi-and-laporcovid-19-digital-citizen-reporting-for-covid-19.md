## Summary

PeduliLindungi is the Indonesian government's official COVID-19 contact tracing and vaccination app, launched March 2020 by Ministry of Communication and Information Technology (MCIT) and Ministry of State-Owned Enterprises (MSOE). Citizens report symptoms, test results, vaccination status, and check-in at venues via QR codes. LaporCOVID-19 is a civil society initiative (Yayasan Warga Berdaya) using WhatsApp/Telegram chatbots for citizen reporting of COVID-19 cases, vaccine access issues, healthcare worker protection, and social aid monitoring. Both are contributory: citizens provide data; government/CSO analyzes.

## Key Points

### Category: Contributory (government-led and CSO-led)
- **PeduliLindungi**: Government-mandated; citizens submit data; centralized analysis; privacy concerns documented
- **LaporCOVID-19**: CSO-led; chatbot on WhatsApp/Telegram; crowdsourced reporting; advocacy outputs

### Impacts
- **PeduliLindungi**: 100M+ downloads; digital vaccination certificates; venue check-in enforcement; integrated with SatuSehat platform
- **LaporCOVID-19**: Documented vaccine inequity, healthcare worker deaths, social aid corruption; published policy briefs; chatbot ver 2.0 (2020)
- **SP4N-LAPOR!**: National complaint system (KemenPANRB/UNDP) repurposed for COVID-19; 240+ COVID reports in 3 months; citizen ideas influenced policy (drive-through testing)

### Methodology
- **PeduliLindungi**: Bluetooth exposure notification + GPS location tracking; QR scanner; facial recognition for mask/temperature; data sent to Telkom analytics endpoint
- **LaporCOVID-19**: Chatbot on WhatsApp/Telegram; citizens report cases, issues; CSO verifies, aggregates, publishes findings
- **SP4N-LAPOR!**: Multi-channel (app, web, SMS); routes complaints to relevant ministry; UNDP/KOICA support

### Points of Improvement
1. **Privacy**: PeduliLindungi collects GPS, name, phone, WiFi MAC - exceeds data minimization (Citizen Lab 2021)
2. **Centralization**: Data stored on government servers; no decentralized protocol (DP-3T, Apple/Google EN)
3. **Digital divide**: Smartphone/app literacy excludes elderly, poor, remote populations
4. **Feedback loop**: Citizens rarely see how their reports translate to action
5. **Sustainability**: LaporCOVID-19 dependent on CSO funding; PeduliLindungi transitioned to SatuSehat
6. **Interoperability**: Multiple parallel systems (PeduliLindungi, SatuSehat, SP4N-LAPOR!, PMI SatuSBM)
