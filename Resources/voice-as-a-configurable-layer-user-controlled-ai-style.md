## Summary

Personality is a design tool, not a fixed identity. Since anthropomorphism's effects are context-dependent [@blut2021], voice should be configurable per domain: concise and factual for high-stakes work, warm for support and companionship settings, with users able to choose their preferred style. A chatty persona is a cognitive tax for neurodivergent users, so default restraint is also an accessibility issue.

The evidence base supports treating voice as a layer rather than a soul. Because trust effects and accountability effects are separable [@oldemburgodemello2026], the persona can be bounded and documented, and because disclosure is a legal requirement [@futureoflifeinstitute2024], warmth must be transparently simulated. Researchers argue engagement must happen across design, development, deployment, use, regulation and policy-making [@peter2025], which implies persona is one controllable parameter within a governed system.

The pragmatist design pattern: neutral, disclosed default; optional styles behind explicit opt-in; context-adaptive switching in high-stakes domains; persona documentation, boundaries and failure modes as first-class artifacts.

## Key Points

- Voice effects are context-dependent, so style should adapt to domain and user preference [@blut2021].
- Trust and accountability effects are separable, allowing bounded, auditable personas [@oldemburgodemello2026].
- Legal disclosure requirements make simulated warmth a transparent convention [@futureoflifeinstitute2024].
