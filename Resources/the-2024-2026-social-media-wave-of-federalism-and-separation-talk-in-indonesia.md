## Summary

Since late 2024 a wave of social media content advocating federalism or separation has circulated in Indonesia, peaking around the nationwide protests of August-September 2025. The most visible layer is speculative entertainment content: TikTok and YouTube videos asking "WHAT IF Indonesia became a federal state?", often AI-generated, and a dedicated hashtag #indonesiafederal used by accounts such as the TikTok page "indonesia.menuju.federal", which pairs the tag with #riau [@tiktok2025; @abadi2025]. Counter-content also circulates (e.g., reels titled "Kenapa sistem federal gak cocok buat Indonesia"), showing the discourse is contested rather than one-directional.

A more organized layer emerged in Riau. After four public discussion forums on "national injustice" (the last on 25 September 2025 at Wareh Kopi, Pekanbaru), regional figures led by activist Said Lukman declared the "Manifesto Riau menuju Sistem Negara Federal dalam lingkup NKRI" on 26 September 2025, under the umbrella of the Forum Aspirasi Negara Federal Indonesia [@lukman2025; @nur2025]. The manifesto deliberately frames federalism as reorganization *within* the unitary state. Parallel drives in the same period include the "Daerah Istimewa Riau" (DIR) movement led by the Malay Customary Council (LAMR) seeking Aceh-style special autonomy, and a five-district push (Dumai, Bengkalis, Kepulauan Meranti, Rokan Hilir, Siak) for a new "Riau Pesisir" province [@abadi2025].

The wave sits inside a broader 2025-2026 atmosphere of viral discontent hashtags (#IndonesiaGelap, #KaburAjaDulu, talk of dissolving the DPR, "Reformasi Jilid II") that followed the August-September 2025 protest movement over economic hardship and a lawmakers' housing-allowance proposal [@lai2026]. Politically, the reaction has been sharp: a former BIN officer claimed in August 2025 that secret meetings planned a "Riau Merdeka" proclamation before Independence Day — an unverified accusation that nonetheless fed public alarm [@sariyem2025].

## Key Points

- Speculative AI "WHAT IF federal" videos and the #indonesiafederal hashtag form the viral layer.
- Organized layer: Riau "Manifesto Federal" (26 Sep 2025), Forum Aspirasi Negara Federal Indonesia, mostly framed as federalism within NKRI.
- Related drives: Daerah Istimewa Riau (LAMR) and the Riau Pesisir pemekaran proposal.
- Peak attention coincided with the Aug-Sep 2025 protests and the 2025-2026 wave of discontent hashtags.
- Unverified "hidden plot" claims (e.g., Riau Merdeka) circulate alongside the discourse.
