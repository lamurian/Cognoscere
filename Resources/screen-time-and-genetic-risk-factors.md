## Summary

Emerging research identifies genetic polymorphisms that interact with screen time to influence health outcomes. The FTO gene polymorphism (rs9939609) interacts with screen time: children with the AA genotype who spend more than 3.8 hours/day on screens show increased waist circumference, while those with 1-3 hours/day do not @gonzlezprez2025. This suggests lifestyle moderates genetic predisposition to obesity.

The CACNA1D gene variant (rs9810888) was associated with blood pressure alterations in children with screen exposure exceeding 2 hours/day. Children carrying the GG genotype had higher diastolic blood pressure (β=5.49, SE=2.18, P=0.012) and mean arterial pressure (β=4.80, SE=1.70, P=0.017) @gonzlezprez2025.

The DRD2 gene polymorphism (rs1800497) reduces dopamine receptor density, making carriers more susceptible to impulsive-addictive behaviors. Adolescents with this allele may use video games as a compensatory mechanism for dopaminergic system deficiencies @gonzlezprez2025. A Mendelian randomization study confirmed that prolonged mobile phone use (OR 1.848) and TV watching (OR 2.104) significantly increased ADHD risk regardless of specific genetic variants @gonzlezprez2025.

## Key Points

- FTO gene (rs9939609) interacts with 3.8+ hours/day screen time for obesity
- CACNA1D variant (rs9810888) linked to blood pressure with 2+ hours/day screen time
- DRD2 polymorphism (rs1800497) increases addiction susceptibility
- ~10% of screen time effects on attention may be genetically mediated
- Genetic variants not yet used in clinical diagnostics for screen time guidance
