## Summary

Desa Siaga TB (Tuberculosis Alert Villages) is a Ministry of Health strategy to eliminate TB by 2030 through community-based case finding and treatment support. Village cadres (kader), community leaders, and health volunteers screen for TB symptoms (cough >2 weeks, weight loss, night sweats), refer suspects to Puskesmas, and support treatment adherence for 6+ months. The approach integrates with Posyandu, village governance, and religious/community forums to reduce stigma.

## Key Points

### Category: Collaborative
- **Co-design**: Village cadres, leaders, health workers jointly design screening, education, support protocols
- **Shared authority**: Village government allocates Village Fund (Dana Desa) for TB activities
- **Bidirectional linkage**: Cadres report to Puskesmas; Puskesmas provides supervision, drugs, lab confirmation

### Impacts
- Community-based interventions increase case detection (global evidence)
- Addresses key gap: thousands of undetected TB cases in facility-only systems
- Reduces stigma through community forums, religious studies, Posyandu education
- Treatment adherence support prevents drug resistance
- Aligns with national TB elimination target (2030)

### Methodology
- **Cadre role**: Screen household contacts, educate on symptoms, refer to Puskesmas, support DOTS
- **Village integration**: TB program in village development agenda; Village Fund allocation
- **Multi-stakeholder**: Village government, religious leaders, PKK, youth groups, Puskesmas
- **Reporting**: Linked to national TB information system (SITB)
- **Training**: Technical (symptom recognition) + interpersonal (stigma reduction, communication)

### Points of Improvement
1. **Cadre capacity**: Uneven training; not all villages have TB-trained cadres
2. **Funding**: Reliant on short-term projects; needs sustained Village Fund commitment
3. **Cross-sector coordination**: Health sector cannot do alone; needs education, social, religious sectors
4. **Reporting integration**: CBS data must link to SITB; current systems fragmented
5. **Stigma measurement**: No standardized metrics for stigma reduction impact
6. **Urban adaptation**: Model designed for villages; urban slums need different approach
