## Summary

For Indonesian ETF investors, the app choice depends on whether you want Indonesian or US ETFs, your trading frequency, and budget. Pluang (12M+ users) excels at US ETFs with 650+ options, 4x leverage, and 24-hour trading. However, the user reports Pluang's topup fees have recently increased and the process has become difficult. GoTrade Indonesia emerges as the best alternative for small monthly US ETF investments, with free bank transfer topups, fractional shares from $1, and competitive 0.3% trading fees.

## Best App for ETF Investing by Use Case

**For monthly US ETF investing (IDR 800K/~$45)** — GoTrade Indonesia is the best option: free bank transfer topup, fractional shares from $1, 0.3% trading fee (min $0.10), and Bappebti-regulated. Total monthly cost: ~$0.14 per $45 buy (0.3%).

**For US ETFs with largest selection (650+)** — Pluang offers the broadest US ETF selection and 24-hour trading, but the user reports topup fees have recently increased and the process has become burdensome. Ajaib Alpha offers US ETFs but with full-share pricing on many tickers.

**For IDX ETFs with lowest fees** — Stockbit and Ajaib are tied at 0.15% buy / 0.25% sell. Stockbit wins on transaction frequency and community features. Ajaib wins on product breadth (crypto, US stocks).

**For passive ETF investing** — GoTrade offers the simplest monthly DCA for US ETFs. For IDX ETFs, use Ajaib or Stockbit. Bibit requires Stockbit integration for ETFs which adds friction.

## Verdict: Best Alternatives to Pluang (now that Pluang topups are expensive)

**For US ETFs with free topup** — GoTrade Indonesia. Free bank transfer topups, fractional shares, $1 minimum, and Bappebti-regulated locally. Side benefit: dividend reinvestment feature.

**For Indonesian ETFs** — Stockbit remains best (0.15%/0.25%, #1 by transaction volume, strong community).

**For one app covering both** — Ajaib (with Ajaib Alpha for US stocks/ETFs) covers IDX ETFs, US ETFs, crypto, and stocks in one ecosystem.

**For active traders** — IPOT offers professional trading tools but at higher fees (0.19%/0.29%).

## Key Considerations

- GoTrade's $2 withdrawal fee means you should minimize withdrawals (hold long-term, withdraw only at meaningful portfolio sizes)
- GoTrade has a 3-trade-per-5-day limit for regular users — designed for investing rather than day trading
- All Indonesian-regulated apps (OJK/Bappebti) use segregated accounts for fund safety
- Robinhood's acquisition of Buana Capital (H1 2026) will bring a new major US broker to Indonesia soon
