## Summary

The total economic impact of high screen time in Dutch children operates through three main pathways: (1) direct healthcare costs for screen-time-associated conditions, (2) education system costs, and (3) parental productivity losses. Using available European evidence adjusted for Netherlands pricing via PPP, the estimated annual per-child economic burden ranges from €3,400 to €9,500 depending on the condition profile and severity.

For a child with ADHD (the most costly screen-time-associated condition), the total societal cost in the Netherlands is estimated at €2,800-€3,000 per year (direct healthcare + education + caregiving). For a child with obesity, the annualized lifetime cost is approximately €600-€850 per year. For children with comorbid conditions (ADHD + obesity + anxiety/depression), the combined annual burden could reach €5,000-€9,500 per child. These figures are consistent with the broader European evidence: the JANPA study found lifetime costs of €16,000-€23,000 per child with overweight/obesity, while the global ADHD literature reports $831-$20,538 per person per year.

The cut-off for 'high' screen time, above which these economic costs become significant, is approximately 3 hours per day of recreational use for school-age children and 2+ hours for adolescents. At these thresholds, cardiometabolic risk begins to accumulate measurably, and the associated conditions (obesity, ADHD symptoms, sleep disorders, anxiety) generate healthcare and societal costs. The Netherlands' universal healthcare system covers most direct treatment costs, but the broader societal burden (education, caregiving, productivity) falls largely on families and the education system. Prevention through screen time reduction could yield substantial savings: the JANPA model suggests a 5% reduction in childhood BMI alone would save over €1 billion in lifetime costs per country cohort.

## Key Points

| Condition | Annual Per-Child Cost (Netherlands, PPP-adjusted) |
|-----------|--------------------------------------------------|
| ADHD (total societal) | €2,800-€3,000 |
| Obesity (annualized lifetime) | €600-€850 |
| Anxiety/Depression (treatment) | €200-€5,000 |
| Comorbid (ADHD + obesity + anxiety) | €5,000-€9,500 |

- **High screen time threshold**: 3+ hours/day recreational (school-age), 2+ hours/day (adolescents)
- **Direct healthcare coverage**: basisverzekering covers contracted care after €385 deductible
- **Largest unmeasured cost**: educational system burden (special education, grade retention)
- **Prevention potential**: 5% BMI reduction saves >€1 billion lifetime per country cohort
- **National prevalence of high screen time**: ~50-60% of Dutch children exceed WHO guidelines
