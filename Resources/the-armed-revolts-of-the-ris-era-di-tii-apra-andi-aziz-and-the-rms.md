## Summary

During the roughly eight months of the United States of Indonesia (RIS, 27 December 1949 – 17 August 1950), four armed movements challenged the federal state. Three of them — APRA, the Andi Aziz revolt, and the RMS — fought explicitly to preserve the federal structure, while Darul Islam (DI/TII) was an ideological revolt for an Islamic state that spanned the era [@wikipediacontributors2025j].

**Darul Islam / Tentara Islam Indonesia (DI/TII)** — Islamist insurgency led by Kartosuwiryo that proclaimed the "Islamic State of Indonesia" in West Java on 7 August 1949, months before the RIS formally began, as a protest against the Renville Agreement that had ceded West Java to the Dutch. It did not disband after the December 1949 transfer of sovereignty and fought through the RIS era until Kartosuwiryo's capture in 1962 [@wikipediacontributors2025j]. Its target was the secular republic, not the federal form as such.

**APRA coup (22–23 January 1950)** — RaymonWesterling's Legion of the Just Ruler (Angkatan Perang Ratu Adil) seized Bandung and infiltrated Jakarta to overthrow the government and preserve the RIS, in conspiracy with federalist cabinet member Sultan Hamid II of Pontianak. The coup failed within a day; APRA ceased to function by February 1950 [@wikipediacontributors2025h].

**Andi Aziz / Makassar Uprising (5–21 April 1950)** — KNIL captain Andi Aziz seized Makassar in the State of East Indonesia, invoking the Federal Constitution to resist the state's integration into the Republic. He was arrested on 14 April while negotiating in Jakarta, and troops under Suharto and Kawilarang occupied Makassar by 21 April [@wikipediacontributors2025i].

**Republic of South Maluku / RMS (25 April 1950)** — demobilised Ambonese KNIL soldiers proclaimed an independent Republic of South Maluku at Ambon, arguing the Round Table federal treaty had been broken by the return to a unitary state. Ambon fell to Indonesian forces in November 1950, a guerrilla war continued on Ceram until 1962, and a government-in-exile has operated in the Netherlands since 1966 [@wikipediacontributors2025k].

## Key Points

- DI/TII — proclaimed 7 Aug 1949 (pre-RIS), active throughout the era, ended 1962 — Islamic-state revolt.
- APRA — 22–23 Jan 1950 — pro-federal coup, Bandung and Jakarta.
- Andi Aziz — 5–21 Apr 1950 — pro-federal revolt, Makassar (State of East Indonesia).
- RMS — 25 Apr 1950 — pro-federal secession, Ambon; guerrilla until 1962.
- Three of the four revolts were pro-federal; DI/TII attacked the state's ideological basis instead.
