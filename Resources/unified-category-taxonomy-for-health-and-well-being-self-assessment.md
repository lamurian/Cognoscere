## Summary

Across six major health and well-being frameworks — WHO ICF [@kostanjsek2011], PROMIS [@cella2007], PERMA [@seligman2018], EQ-5D/SF-36 [@euroqolgroup1990; @ware1992], OECD Better Life Index [@oecd2020], and Ryff/WHO-5 [@ryff1995; @topp2015] — a consistent pattern of seven superordinate categories emerges for grouping self-assessment domains.

**Category 1: Physical Health.** This covers bodily functioning, symptoms, and physical sensations. Includes: physical function/mobility, pain, fatigue/energy, sleep, sensory functions. Frameworks: ICF (Body Functions), PROMIS (Physical Health), SF-36 (Physical Functioning, Bodily Pain), EQ-5D (Mobility, Pain/Discomfort), OECD (Health Status).

**Category 2: Mental and Emotional Health.** Covers cognitive and affective states, both positive and negative. Includes: depression, anxiety, anger, positive affect, cognitive function, psychological well-being. Frameworks: PROMIS (Mental Health), SF-36 (Mental Health, Role Emotional), EQ-5D (Anxiety/Depression), WHO-5 (positive mood/vitality), Ryff PWB (6 eudaimonic dimensions), PERMA (Positive Emotion).

**Category 3: Social Health and Relationships.** Addresses social connectedness, support networks, and social role participation. Includes: social support, social isolation, relationships quality, community belonging, social role functioning. Frameworks: PROMIS (Social Health), PERMA (Relationships), OECD (Social Connections), ICF (Activities & Participation), SF-36 (Social Functioning).

**Category 4: Functional Capacity and Daily Life.** Focuses on the ability to perform activities of daily living, work, and life roles. Includes: self-care, usual activities, work/school functioning, mobility in environment, independence. Frameworks: ICF (Activities & Participation), EQ-5D (Self-Care, Usual Activities), PROMIS (Physical Function), SF-36 (Role Physical, Role Emotional).

**Category 5: Meaning, Purpose, and Fulfilment.** Captures eudaimonic aspects of well-being — meaning, engagement, accomplishment, personal growth. Includes: purpose in life, engagement/flow, accomplishment/mastery, personal growth, self-acceptance, environmental mastery. Frameworks: PERMA (Meaning, Engagement, Accomplishment), Ryff PWB (Purpose in Life, Personal Growth, Self-Acceptance, Autonomy), OECD (Life Satisfaction).

**Category 6: Health Behaviours and Lifestyle.** Addresses modifiable behaviours that influence health and well-being. Includes: physical activity/exercise, nutrition, substance use, sleep hygiene, health service utilisation, risk behaviours. Frameworks: OECD (Work-Life Balance), partially covered by ICF environmental factors and PROMIS (less directly). Many instruments treat these as separate health determinants rather than outcomes.

**Category 7: Environmental and Contextual Factors.** Covers external conditions shaping health and well-being. Includes: housing, income/wealth, education, environmental quality, safety/security, civic engagement, healthcare access, social determinants. Frameworks: OECD (Housing, Income, Education, Environment, Safety, Civic Engagement), ICF (Environmental Factors), and increasingly integrated into social determinants of health frameworks.

## Recommended Use

For practical self-assessment grouping, the 7-category taxonomy provides comprehensive coverage of all domains captured by existing instruments. The categories are non-overlapping when applied as primary groupings, though some instruments map to multiple categories (e.g., SF-36 Vitality overlaps Physical and Mental categories). Choose categories based on assessment purpose: clinical screening (focus on 1-4), well-being promotion (focus on 4-6), or population health monitoring (include 7).
