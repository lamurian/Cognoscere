## Summary

The question of "seven categories of health" in the WHO ICF context requires clarification. The ICF itself does not define exactly seven categories — it has 3 components (Body Functions/Structures, Activities/Participation, Environmental Factors) containing 30 chapters total [@worldhealthorganization2001]. However, when the ICF is synthesized with five other major health and well-being frameworks (PROMIS, PERMA, EQ-5D/SF-36, OECD Better Life Index, and Ryff/WHO-5), a consistent pattern of seven superordinate categories emerges [@kostanjsek2011; @cella2007; @seligman2018; @euroqolgroup1990; @ware1992; @oecd2020; @ryff1995; @topp2015].

## The Seven Categories

**1. Physical Health** — Bodily functioning, symptoms, and physical sensations. Maps to ICF Body Functions, PROMIS Physical Health, SF-36 Physical Functioning/Bodily Pain, EQ-5D Mobility/Pain/Discomfort.

**2. Mental and Emotional Health** — Cognitive and affective states (depression, anxiety, positive affect, cognitive function). Maps to PROMIS Mental Health, EQ-5D Anxiety/Depression, WHO-5, Ryff PWB dimensions, PERMA Positive Emotion.

**3. Social Health and Relationships** — Social connectedness, support networks, social role participation. Maps to PROMIS Social Health, PERMA Relationships, OECD Social Connections, ICF Activities and Participation, SF-36 Social Functioning.

**4. Functional Capacity and Daily Life** — Ability to perform activities of daily living, work, and life roles. Maps to ICF Activities and Participation, EQ-5D Self-Care/Usual Activities, PROMIS Physical Function.

**5. Meaning, Purpose, and Fulfilment** — Eudaimonic well-being: purpose, engagement, accomplishment, growth. Maps to PERMA (Meaning, Engagement, Accomplishment), Ryff PWB (Purpose in Life, Personal Growth, Self-Acceptance), OECD Life Satisfaction.

**6. Health Behaviours and Lifestyle** — Modifiable behaviours influencing health: physical activity, nutrition, substance use, sleep hygiene. Partially covered by OECD Work-Life Balance and ICF environmental factors.

**7. Environmental and Contextual Factors** — External conditions: housing, income, education, safety, healthcare access, social determinants. Maps to OECD (Housing, Income, Education, Environment, Safety, Civic Engagement) and ICF Environmental Factors.

## ICF Structure vs. Seven-Category Synthesis

The ICF organises health information into two parts: (a) Functioning and Disability — Body Functions, Body Structures, Activities and Participation; and (b) Contextual Factors — Environmental Factors and Personal Factors (not yet classified). Each component has chapters (8 for Body Functions, 8 for Body Structures, 9 for Activities and Participation, 5 for Environmental Factors) with alphanumeric codes at varying granularity [@worldhealthorganization2001].

The seven-category taxonomy is a higher-level synthesis that groups ICF domains alongside domains from other frameworks. For clinical screening, focus on categories 1-4; for well-being promotion, focus on 4-6; for population health monitoring, include all seven.
