## Summary

Bibit (PT Bibit Tumbuh Bersama) is an Indonesian robo-advisor investment platform launched in 2017, focusing primarily on mutual funds (reksa dana), SBN (government bonds), and Fixed Rate (FR) bonds. It operates as an APERD (Agen Penjual Efek Reksa Dana) licensed by OJK under SK KEP-14/PM.21/2017. In 2025, Bibit recorded 2 million app downloads (+37% YoY) with 1.7 million investment portfolios. It serves as a passive investment vehicle for users who want automated portfolio management. Bibit also provides stock and ETF trading via integration with Stockbit Sekuritas (users must upgrade to Bibit Plus and open an RDN with Stockbit).

## ETF Availability

Bibit does not offer direct ETF trading. ETF trading is available only through the Bibit Plus upgrade, which creates an account with PT Stockbit Sekuritas Digital (kode broker CH). The transaction executes through Stockbit's platform, not Bibit's. For users primarily interested in ETFs, this adds friction — you effectively need a Stockbit account to trade ETFs via Bibit.

## Fee Breakdown

**Mutual funds** (Bibit's core product):
- Buy/sell: FREE (no commission)
- Only management fees charged by the mutual fund manager (included in NAV)
- Top reksa dana feature automatically curates top funds

**SBN (Government Bonds)**:
- No transaction fee
- Only interbank transfer fee if your bank differs from RDN

**Stocks/ETFs** (via Stockbit Sekuritas integration):
- Buy: 0.15%
- Sell: 0.25%
- PPh final 0.1% on sell

**Topup fees**:
- RDN Wallet or Bank Jago: FREE
- GoPay, Shopee Pay, LinkAja: Rp 2,500 per topup
- Virtual Account BCA: free 4x/month, then Rp 4,600
- Virtual Account other banks: Rp 3,100
- Interbank withdrawal transfer: Rp 2,900 (if bank ≠ RDN bank)

**Other fees**:
- Live Datafeed: Rp 15,000/month if monthly stock transaction value > Rp 20M (saldo Rp 20-100M)
- Materai Rp 10,000 for daily transactions exceeding Rp 10M

## Topup Methods

Bibit offers comprehensive topup options:
- Bank Jago integration (free and instant)
- Virtual Account (BCA, Mandiri, BNI, BRI, etc.)
- E-wallet (GoPay, Shopee Pay, LinkAja)
- RDN Wallet

This diversity of topup methods is a key advantage — Bibit is more accessible than pure securities apps which typically only support bank transfer.

## User Reviews

Bibit scores 4.8/5 on both Google Play Store and Apple App Store, the highest rating among Indonesian investment apps. Users consistently praise:
- Extremely simple and clean interface
- Robo-advisor removes complexity for beginners
- Educational content (Bibit Academy)
- Low minimum investment (Rp 10,000 for mutual funds)
- Auto-debit for Dollar Cost Averaging

Common complaints (per May 2026 Rankia review):
- Registration and verification process can be slow (up to 1x24 hours)
- Recent complaints about withdrawal delays
- Stock/ETF trading requires Stockbit account (added friction)
- Not suitable for active traders (no real-time data, no technical analysis tools)
- Mutual fund returns depend on fund manager performance
