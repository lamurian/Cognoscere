## Summary

Cirqle Stamp (usecirqle.com) is a QR-based digital loyalty program for small businesses that replaces physical punch cards with smartphone wallet passes. It targets merchants who want a simple, no-POS, no-app-download loyalty solution — set up in 5 minutes, free to start.

The core mechanic: customers scan a QR code at the counter to receive a digital stamp card in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet. On each return visit, staff scan the customer's wallet pass to add a stamp. When the threshold is reached (e.g., 10 stamps), a reward is automatically unlocked. No app download is required for customers since wallet apps are pre-installed on all modern smartphones.

Cirqle Stamp differentiates itself from multi-brand loyalty ecosystems (like cirqleapp.com's F&B loyalty ecosystem) by targeting single-location small businesses with a frictionless onboarding experience. The QR-based, wallet-native approach eliminates fraud (each stamp is issued by staff scan, not self-stamped), eliminates printing costs, and provides merchants with basic analytics on visit frequency and customer engagement.

## Key Points

- QR-based enrollment: customer scans code, digital card added to Apple/Google Wallet in seconds
- No POS integration needed: staff scan wallet passes using any smartphone camera
- No app download for customers: wallet apps are native on every iPhone and Android
- Fraud-proof: stamps only issued by staff scan, preventing self-stamping or duplication
- Free to start with tiered pricing for advanced features
- Provides visit frequency data and engagement metrics via merchant dashboard
