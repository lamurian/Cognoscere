## Summary

QR-based digital loyalty stamp programs are the digital evolution of the classic paper punch card. Instead of carrying a physical card, customers receive a digital pass in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet after scanning a QR code. On each subsequent visit, staff scan the customer's wallet pass to add a stamp, and rewards unlock automatically when thresholds are met.

### How It Works

**Enrollment.** The customer scans a QR code displayed at the counter, on a table tent, or via a link. This creates a unique digital pass linked to their phone and adds it to their native wallet app (Apple Wallet or Google Wallet). The process takes under 10 seconds and requires no app download.

**Earning stamps.** On each return visit, staff scan the customer's wallet pass using a standard smartphone camera or tablet. The system records the visit timestamp, updates the stamp count in real time, and the customer sees their progress immediately on the pass.

**Redemption.** When the customer reaches the reward threshold (typically 8-10 stamps), the system triggers a push notification. The customer shows their pass at checkout, staff scan it, and the reward is applied.

**Ongoing engagement.** Merchants can send push notifications directly to customers' lock screens — promotions, birthday offers, win-back campaigns for inactive customers.

### Advantages Over Paper Punch Cards

Paper punch cards carry hidden costs: printing (up to $0.50/card), fraud from self-stamping or duplication (3-5% of loyalty giveaways), zero customer data collection, and no re-engagement channel. Digital wallet programs solve all four. The "endowed progress effect" — customers who can see their progress toward a goal are significantly more likely to complete it — is built into the wallet pass design, which constantly displays stamp count.

### Business Impact

According to research cited by Revio, members of stamp-based programs visit 20-30% more frequently than non-members. Wallet-based enrollment converts at 2-3x the rate of app-based enrollment because it removes the download step. A 5% increase in customer retention can increase profits by 25-95% (Bain & Company). QR-based programs also collect first-party data — visit frequency, engagement patterns, preferences — which merchants own and can use for targeted marketing.

### Key Points

- Four-step process: QR enrollment → wallet pass → staff scan to stamp → auto-reward at threshold
- No app download — Apple/Google Wallet are pre-installed on all modern smartphones
- Wallet passes show stamp count constantly, driving the endowed progress effect
- Fraud-proof: stamps only issued by staff-controlled QR scans, not self-stamped
- Merchant dashboard provides visit data, active customer counts, and engagement metrics
- 2-3x higher enrollment vs app-based loyalty programs (Airship)
- Members visit 20-30% more frequently than non-members (Bond Brand Loyalty)
