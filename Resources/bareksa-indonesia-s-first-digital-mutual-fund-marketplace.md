## Summary

Bareksa (PT Bareksa Portal Investasi) is Indonesia's first integrated digital investment marketplace, founded on 17 February 2013 by Karaniya Dharmasaputra and Ady F. Pangerang (former Viva Media executives). It pioneered online mutual fund sales in Indonesia, receiving the first OJK license for a fintech to sell mutual funds directly in 2016. Bareksa operates as a marketplace connecting multiple asset management companies (92 registered as of 2019, with Rp 1.8 trillion in total assets under management). It was also one of the first fintech platforms appointed by the government to sell SBN (government bonds) online in 2018. Grab took an undisclosed stake in Bareksa to integrate mutual fund investments via OVO.

## ETF Availability

Bareksa is primarily a mutual fund and SBN marketplace. It does not offer direct ETF trading on its platform. For ETF exposure, users would need a separate securities account (e.g., Stockbit, Ajaib, IPOT). Bareksa also provides stock data and analysis tools, but full stock/ETF trading is not its core offering.

## Fee Breakdown

**Mutual funds** (core product):
- Buy/sell: FREE (no transaction commission)
- Only management fees charged by the asset manager (included in NAV)

**SBN (Government Bonds)**:
- No transaction fee
- Free purchase during SBN offering periods

**Other products** (gold, etc.): Varies by product

**Topup fees**:
- Bank transfer to RDN: free
- Specific VA/e-wallet fees not widely documented

## Topup Methods

Bareksa supports bank transfer to the RDN (Rekening Dana Nasabah) at partner banks. The integration with OVO and Grab provides potential alternative topup channels, though specific fee details for e-wallet topups are not widely publicized.

## User Reviews

Bareksa's key strengths according to user feedback:
- Largest selection of mutual fund products (92+ asset managers)
- Product comparison tools valuable for research
- Simulator and investment calculator tools
- Trusted brand as marketplace pioneer
- Good SBN purchasing experience
- One account for multiple products

Common complaints:
- Interface is less polished than Bibit or Ajaib
- Customer service can be slow
- No robo-advisor feature (user must choose funds manually)
- No direct stock or ETF trading
- Fewer marketing promotions compared to competitors
- Some users report slow fund redemption (1-7 working days, standard for industry)

## Key Facts

- Launch year: 17 February 2013
- Founders: Karaniya Dharmasaputra, Ady F. Pangerang
- Type: Marketplace (mutual funds, SBN, gold)
- Regulated by: OJK
- First fintech to receive OJK mutual fund sales license (2016)
- 92+ asset managers registered (2019)
- AUM: Rp 1.8 trillion (March 2019)
- Grab/OVO strategic investor
- Minimum investment: Rp 10,000
- ETF: Not available directly (no stock/ETF trading)
- Best for: Mutual fund comparison shopping and SBN purchasing
