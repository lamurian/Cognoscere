## Summary

Stockbit is Indonesia's leading social investing platform that combines stock trading with a community-driven social feed. Originally launched as a social network for investors, it now operates as PT Stockbit Sekuritas Digital (kode broker CH), a fully licensed securities company under OJK. Stockbit dominates the Indonesian retail equities space as the #1 securities firm by transaction frequency and #3 by transaction volume among 95 brokerages. In 2025, Stockbit posted explosive growth: IDR 596.5 billion net revenue (+395% YoY) and IDR 243.6 billion net profit (+544% YoY).

## ETF Availability

Stockbit offers trading in all Indonesian ETFs listed on IDX. Unlike Ajaib, Stockbit does not provide US ETFs directly — its focus is Indonesian equities and ETFs. For US stocks/ETFs, users would need another platform.

## Fee Breakdown

**Stock/ETF transaction fees**:
- Buy: 0.15% (competitive, some promo rates as low as 0.10%)
- Sell: 0.25% (some promo rates as low as 0.20%)
- Fees include broker commission, BEI/KPEI/KSEI levy (0.0433%), and PPN 12%
- PPh final 0.1% applies on sell transactions

**Other fees**:
- No minimum deposit required
- No account maintenance fee
- No topup fee (bank transfer to RDN)

## Topup Methods

Stockbit supports bank transfer topup to the RDN at partner banks. Topup is free of charge. Withdrawal to external accounts incurs standard interbank transfer fees. The platform does not support e-wallet topups (GoPay, OVO, etc.) — only bank transfer to RDN.

## User Reviews

Stockbit is the dominant retail trading platform in Indonesia. Media Indonesia reports it as the platform with the most users among Indonesian stock investors. Users praise:
- Active community with quality stock analysis shared openly
- Stockbit Academy for educational content
- Clean, ad-free UI/UX
- Chartbit Tools with technical indicators
- Broker Flow feature showing institutional activity
- Desktop app for macOS (rare in Indonesian brokerages)

Common complaints include:
- Social feed can cause information overload
- Order execution can be delayed during high volatility
- Charting is less advanced than IPOT (Indo Premier)
- Limited to Indonesian market (no US stocks)
- Customer support response times vary

Rankia's editorial team (April 2026) tested Stockbit's order execution and found that during normal market conditions, all orders executed instantly. During high volatility, some delays were reported but less severe than Ajaib's.

## Key Facts

- Launch year: ~2015 (social platform), licensed securities ~2020
- Revenue 2025: IDR 596.5 billion (+395% YoY)
- Net profit 2025: IDR 243.6 billion (+544% YoY)
- 35M+ stock transactions Jan-Jul 2025
- #1 by transaction frequency among 95 securities firms
- Top 3 by transaction volume
- Minimum deposit: Rp 0 (none)
- ETF: Indonesian ETFs only (no US ETFs)
- Regulation: OJK (PT Stockbit Sekuritas Digital)
- Key differentiator: social community + trading integration
