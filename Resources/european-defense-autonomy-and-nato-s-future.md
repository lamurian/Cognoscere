## Summary

The transatlantic alliance faces its most serious structural test since the Cold War. The Munich Security Conference 2025 witnessed what media described as "the funeral of the West" — a speech by the American president signaling potential collapse of the transatlantic alliance. The widening gap between Europe and the United States is now apparent in both practical interests and values, with separation "in some form" seemingly inevitable (@bunde2025).

Europe is described as "the first victim of the West's decline," marking the end of an eighty-year era of undisputed U.S. leadership of the Western system. The EU-NATO cooperation debate centers on three realistic scenarios: "Transatlantic NATO Minus" (U.S. remains but reduces military footprint), full European strategic autonomy, and conditional autonomy within NATO. NATO's 2025 Annual Report shows European and Canadian defense spending increased 106% in real terms between 2014 and 2025.

However, skeptics warn that pursuing autonomy could inadvertently weaken the transatlantic bond if perceived as decoupling. The IRSEM analysis argues Trump's first term catalyzed strategic autonomy debates through questioning collective defense within NATO. European defense remains fundamentally dependent on U.S. capabilities in intelligence, surveillance, reconnaissance, and strategic airlift.

The TDC assessment argues a U.S. pivot toward the Indo-Pacific could reshape NATO, test Europe's defense autonomy, and expose gaps in transatlantic deterrence. European security futures across 2026-2035 remain contingent on American strategic commitment, alliance cohesion, and European political capacity to sustain defense expenditures during economic constraints.

## Key Points

- 2025 Munich Security Conference: "funeral of the West" — transatlantic gap widening
- Three scenarios: U.S. footprint reduction, full autonomy, or conditional autonomy within NATO
- European/Canadian defense spending up 106% real terms since 2014
- Europe remains dependent on U.S. ISR and strategic airlift capabilities
- 2026-2035 security futures contingent on U.S. commitment and alliance cohesion
