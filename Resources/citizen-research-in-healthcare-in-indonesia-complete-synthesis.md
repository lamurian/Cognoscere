## Summary

Indonesia hosts a diverse ecosystem of citizen research in healthcare across three citizen science typologies. **Contributory** (citizens as data collectors): Posyandu kader network (500k+ volunteers), Satu Rumah Satu Jumantik dengue larval surveillance (65M household target), PMI/IFRC CBS with SatuSBM app, PeduliLindungi/LaporCOVID-19 digital reporting. **Collaborative** (citizens as partners): Desa Siaga TB village-based case finding, North Sumatra NCD cadre empowerment (co-designed training/referrals), Samarinda PAR for dengue (GEMATRI mother groups, village decree). **Co-created** (citizens as leaders): LaporCOVID-19 civil society platform defining advocacy priorities. Impacts include improved immunization (87%), larva-free index (+15.76 pp), TB case detection (100% CDR in pilot), NCD referrals (+232%), early dengue detection saving lives. Cross-cutting improvements needed: digital interoperability, standardized incentives, competency-based training, formal community-to-facility reporting SOPs, Village Fund integration, equity-focused design, CBS system evaluation metrics, and regulatory harmonization.

## Key Points

### Typology Distribution and Characteristics

| Typology | Projects | Citizen Role | Governance |
|----------|----------|--------------|------------|
| **Contributory** | Posyandu, Jumantik, PMI CBS, PeduliLindungi | Data collection per protocol | Top-down (MoH, PMI, Govt) |
| **Collaborative** | Desa Siaga TB, North Sumatra NCD, Samarinda PAR | Co-design protocols, tools, referral | Shared (village + Puskesmas + community) |
| **Co-created** | LaporCOVID-19 advocacy | Define questions, methods, outputs | Bottom-up (CSO coalition) |

### Documented Impacts by Project

1. **Posyandu Kader**: 87% immunization coverage; 81% child weighing; MCH mortality decline
2. **Jumantik/G1R1J**: 33,392 monitors in Jakarta; larva-free index 50.67%→66.43% (Samarinda PAR)
3. **PMI CBS/SatuSBM**: High predictive accuracy (dengue, COVID-19, TB, leptospirosis); early detection saving lives; Permenko No. 7/2022 legal framework
4. **PeduliLindungi**: 100M+ downloads; digital vaccination certs; SatuSehat integration
5. **LaporCOVID-19**: Documented vaccine inequity, HCW deaths, social aid corruption; policy briefs
6. **Desa Siaga TB**: Community case finding closes detection gap; stigma reduction; Village Fund integration
7. **North Sumatra NCD**: Cadre knowledge +32%, competency +45%, referrals +232% (highest in remote Karo +308%)
8. **Samarinda PAR (GEMATRI)**: Village decree on dengue budget; mother-led groups; murals as health comms

### How They Are Conducted: Common Methodological Threads

- **Volunteer base**: PKK women's groups, PMI volunteers, village-appointed cadres
- **Training**: Short formal (<1 week) + on-the-job; PAR projects use workshops/FGDs/mentoring
- **Supervision**: Puskesmas technical guidance; village accountability; PAR adds researcher facilitation
- **Data flow**: Predominantly paper→Puskesmas; digital pilots (SatuSBM, SOBAT TB, PeduliLindungi) not integrated
- **Incentives**: Minimal transport reimbursement (IDR 300-450k/yr); NGO performance-based for TB; no standardized framework
- **Community engagement**: Gotong royong (mutual cooperation), 3M Plus, Clean Saturday, Posyandu sessions, religious/community forums

### Evaluation: Points of Improvement (Prioritized)

1. **Digital Integration**: Single national CBS data standard; API interoperability; retire parallel systems
2. **Incentive Reform**: Performance-linked, standardized compensation; kader career pathway; WHO-aligned professionalization
3. **Training Overhaul**: Competency-based certification; regular refreshers; digital literacy; co-designed curricula
4. **Reporting SOPs**: Simple case definitions + guidelines for ALL priority diseases (VPDs, leptospirosis, AFP)
5. **Local Financing**: Mandate health allocation in Village Fund (Dana Desa); leverage CSR in resource-rich regions
6. **Equity by Design**: SMS/USSD/voice channels; male cadre recruitment; Eastern Indonesia focus
7. **CBS System M&E**: Monitor acceptability, simplicity, flexibility, timeliness — not just validity
8. **Regulatory Harmony**: Implement Permenko No. 7/2022 consistently across village/district/province
